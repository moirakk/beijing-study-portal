# Unit 1

标签：#Module5 #LookAfterYourself #建议句型 #听说课 ｜ 课文：We'd better get you to hospital.

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 5 Look after yourself 的听说课。情境：Betty 在打篮球时摔伤了脚，同学们围过来查看伤情——先询问哪里疼、能不能站起来，然后商量处理办法：We'd **better get you to hospital**.（我们最好送你去医院。）随后是就医对话：医生检查、询问症状并给出休息和用药建议。

语言重点是**had better / should 等建议句型**在"受伤与就医"场景中的运用，以及描述伤病的表达（hurt, painful, X-ray 等）。"健康与安全"是北京中考听说和情景交际的高频话题。

关键句：
- **We'd better get** you **to** hospital. 我们最好送你去医院。
- **Does it hurt?** 疼吗？— It's painful when I stand up.
- You **should stay in bed** and rest for a few days. 你应该卧床休息几天。
- **You'd better not move.** 你最好别动。

## 二、重点词汇与短语

| 词汇 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hurt | v./adj. | 疼痛；受伤（的） | My foot hurts. |
| painful | adj. | 疼痛的 | It's painful to walk. |
| awful | adj. | 糟糕的 | I feel awful today. |
| medicine | n. | 药 | Take the medicine twice a day. |
| X-ray | n. | X 光检查 | You need an X-ray. |
| had better do | 短语 | 最好做 | You'd better see a doctor. |
| get sb. to | 短语 | 把某人送到 | Get him to hospital quickly. |
| stay in bed | 短语 | 卧床 | Stay in bed and rest. |

## 三、重点句型

1. **We'd better do** sth. / **You'd better not do** sth.（最好做/最好别做）
2. **What's wrong / the matter with** you?（询问病情）
3. **You should / shouldn't do** sth.（医嘱建议）
4. **Why not do...? / What about doing...?**（委婉建议）

## 四、语法讲解：建议句型系统

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["提出建议的句型"] --> B["had better do (最好做, 略带警告)"]
    A --> C["should do (应该, 一般建议)"]
    A --> D["Why not do...? = Why don't you do...?"]
    A --> E["What / How about doing...?"]
    A --> F["Let's do... / Shall we do...?"]
    B --> B1["否定: had better not do"]
    C --> C1["否定: shouldn't do"]
```

had better 无人称和时态变化，缩写为 'd better，后接**动词原形**；语气比 should 更急切，常用于当下具体情境。

## 五、易错点

1. had better 否定：❌ had not better do → ✅ had better **not** do。
2. ❌ You'd better **to see** a doctor. → ✅ You'd better **see** a doctor.（接原形）
3. hurt 过去式、过去分词仍为 hurt：I hurt my leg yesterday.
4. Why not **do**...? 后接原形；What about **doing**...? 后接动名词。

## 六、背记要点

- 就医三问：What's wrong with you? / Does it hurt? / How long have you been like this?
- 医嘱三句：Take the medicine three times a day. / Stay in bed. / Drink more water.
- 'd better = had better（不是 would better）。

## 七、自测题

1. 单选：You look pale. You'd better ______ a rest.（A. have  B. to have  C. having  D. had）
2. 单选：It's late. We'd better ______ the road now. The traffic is heavy.（A. not cross  B. not to cross  C. don't cross  D. not crossing）
3. 完成句子：我们最好马上送她去医院。We'd ______ ______ her to hospital at once.
4. 情景交际：同学脚扭伤了，用两个不同句型给出建议。

**答案**：1. A  2. A  3. better get  4. 示例：You'd better not move. / You should put ice on it.
