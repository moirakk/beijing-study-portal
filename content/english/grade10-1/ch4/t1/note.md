# Unit 4 词汇与课文精读（Understanding ideas）

标签：#Unit4 #FriendsForever #友谊 #词汇课 ｜ 课文：Click for a Friend?

## 主题导入

本单元主题是"永恒的友谊"（Friends forever）。课文探讨互联网时代的交友方式：网络朋友（online friends）是否是真朋友？文章呈现正反两方观点并引用调查数据。"网络与人际关系"是北京高考阅读议论文与书面表达（观点类）的热点话题。

## 重点短语与句型

1. **make friends with sb.**：与某人交朋友。
2. **in favour of**：支持，赞成 — Most students are in favour of online friendship.
3. **keep in touch with**：与…保持联系。
4. **It is reported/believed that...**：据报道/据信（议论文常用引证句式）。
5. **compared with...**：与…相比（数据对比句式）。

## 课文精读笔记

- 文体：**议论文/报告文**，结构为"现象引入 → 数据呈现 → 正方观点 → 反方观点 → 平衡结论"。
- 数据语言：A recent survey shows that **the majority of** teenagers have made friends online; **约数表达** more than / nearly / up to 是阅读细节题考点。
- 正方论点：网络打破地域限制（break down geographical barriers）；反方论点：虚拟身份有风险（hidden identity, safety risk）。
- 结论句：Whether online or in real life, true friendship is based on trust and honesty.——观点类作文的万能结论模板。

## 典型例题

**例1**（语法填空）：The majority of students ______ (be) in favour of making friends online, but a few worry about the risks.
**解析**：the majority of + 复数名词，谓语用复数 → are。

**例2**（阅读观点题）：What is the author's attitude towards online friendship?
**解析**：结论段 "Whether online or in real life..." 表明作者持**客观/中立**（objective/balanced）态度，不选 completely supportive。

## 易错点

1. the majority of + 复数名词 → 谓语复数；the majority 单独作主语时可单可复。
2. tend to do（倾向于做），无 tend doing 用法。
3. be loyal to 中 to 是介词。
4. compared with 位于句首作状语时，逻辑主语须是句子主语，避免悬垂：Compared with letters, e-mails are faster.

## 自测题

1. 语法填空：About 60 percent of the students surveyed tend ______ (share) their feelings with online friends.
2. 单选：A true friend will never ______ you when you are in trouble.（A. betray  B. trust  C. benefit  D. click）
3. 完成句子：与面对面交流相比，网络聊天缺少情感温度。______ ______ face-to-face communication, online chatting lacks emotional warmth.
4. 改错：She is loyal for her friends all the time.

**答案**：1. to share  2. A  3. Compared with  4. for → to
