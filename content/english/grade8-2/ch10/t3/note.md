# Unit 3 Language in use

标签：#Module10 #语法总结 #宾语从句综合 #广播话题

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 10 的语法综合课，在"广播与媒体"话题中综合运用**宾语从句**（that / if / whether / 疑问词引导）和 it seems that 句型。课堂任务是设计一档班级广播节目并写一段开场词，或写一篇"我的梦想职业"短文。

节目开场词写作要点：
1. 问候听众并介绍节目（Welcome to...）。
2. 预告内容（I hope you will find that...）。
3. 邀请互动（Please tell us what you think about...）。

关键句：
- I hope **that you will enjoy** our programme. 希望你们喜欢我们的节目。
- Please tell us **what music you like best**. 请告诉我们你最喜欢什么音乐。

## 二、重点词汇

- **national** adj. 全国的  **local** adj. 当地的
- **channel** n. 频道  **audience** n. 观众；听众
- **mobile phone** 手机  **the Internet** 互联网
- **at the moment** 目前  **be on** 播出
- **agree with sb.** 同意某人  **in one's twenties** 在某人二十多岁时
- **not ... any more** 不再

## 三、语法要点

**宾语从句总复习（三类引导词）**

| 从句来源 | 引导词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 陈述句 | that（可省） | I think (that) radio **is** still popular. |
| 一般疑问句 | if / whether | She asked **if** I listened to the radio. |
| 特殊疑问句 | what/when/where/why/how | Tell me **how you make** a programme. |

**共同规则**：陈述语序 + 时态呼应（客观真理除外）。

**疑问词 + to do 简化**：I don't know **what to say**. = I don't know what I should say.

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ He asked me **that** I liked music. → ✅ ...**if/whether** I liked music.
2. ❌ Do you know when **will the programme start**? → ✅ when **the programme will start**
3. agree **with** sb. / agree **to** do sth.，介词勿混。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Could you tell me ______ the news programme starts?（A. that  B. what time  C. if or not）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - The host asked me what music I ______ (like) when I was interviewed.
   - I don't know how ______ (make) a radio programme.
3. 合并句子：How does he host the show? I wonder. → I wonder ______ ______ hosts the show.
4. 书面表达（4句）：写一段班级广播开场词，须含一个 that 从句和一个疑问词从句。

**答案**：1. B  2. liked; to make  3. how he  4. 示例：Welcome to our class radio show! I hope that you will enjoy the music today. Please tell us what songs you want to hear. We are waiting for your messages!
