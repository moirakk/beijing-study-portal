# Unit 2 It seemed that they were speaking to me in person

标签：#Module10 #阅读课 #成长故事 #it seems that

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 10 的阅读课，讲述一个少年与广播结缘的成长故事：作者小时候躺在床上听广播，感觉播音员就像在亲自对他说话（It seemed that they were speaking to me in person）；九岁时他在一家小电台得到第一份"工作"，后来通过不断练习，终于成为一名真正的电台播音员。

阅读时按时间线梳理成长历程：童年听广播 → 九岁进电台帮忙 → 少年时主持节目 → 长大后实现梦想。体会文章"兴趣 + 坚持 = 成功"的主题，注意 **It seemed that...** 句型的表达效果。

关键句：
- **It seemed that** they were speaking to me **in person**. 他们仿佛在亲自跟我说话。
- **By the age of** ten, I was hosting a small programme. 到十岁时，我已在主持一个小节目。
- My dream **finally came true**. 我的梦想终于实现了。

## 二、重点词汇

- **seem** v. 似乎  **in person** 亲自
- **truck** n. 卡车  **avenue** n. 大道
- **article** n. 文章  **newspaper** n. 报纸
- **host** v. 主持 n. 主持人  **weekly** adj. 每周的
- **by the age of** 到……岁时  **ask for** 请求
- **look through** 浏览  **as if** 好像
- **come true** 实现

## 三、语法要点

**seem 的三种句型**

1. **It seems/seemed that** + 从句：It seemed that he knew the answer.
2. **sb./sth. seem(s) to do**：He seemed **to know** the answer.
3. **seem + 形容词**：She seems **happy** today.

**by + 时间点**（到……为止），常与进行时/完成时连用：
- **By the age of** ten, he was hosting a programme.
- **By** last year, I had learned 2,000 words.

## 四、易错点

1. It seems that... 中 it 是形式主语，不可换成其他词。
2. ❌ He seems **knowing**... → ✅ He seems **to know**...
3. in person（亲自）vs. in public（公开地）：I want to thank him **in person**.

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：It seemed that the radio host ______ to me in person.（A. speaks  B. was speaking  C. is speaking）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - She seems ______ (be) interested in hosting programmes.
   - His dream of becoming a reporter finally came ______ (true).
3. 同义句转换：It seems that Tom likes the new programme. = Tom seems ______ ______ the new programme.
4. 汉译英：到九岁时，他似乎已经爱上了广播。

**答案**：1. B  2. to be; true  3. to like  4. By the age of nine, it seemed that he had fallen in love with the radio.
