# Revision module B

标签：#复习模块B #Module6-10 #期末复习 #宾语从句

## 一、复习范围

Revision module B 综合复习 Module 6-10 的核心内容，是期末考试前的系统梳理：

1. **Module 6** 双宾语结构（give sb. sth. / give sth. to sb.）；动名词作主语
2. **Module 7** 祈使句；祈使句 + and/or；感叹句（What.../How...）
3. **Module 8** 过去进行时；when/while；so/such...that；hardly
4. **Module 9** 宾语从句：that 与 if/whether 引导；时态呼应
5. **Module 10** 疑问词引导的宾语从句；it seems that；hope 句型

复习方法：先按模块过一遍语法要点和易错题，再做宾语从句专项（语序 + 时态两大考点），最后限时写一篇话题作文（爱好 / 友谊 / 假期经历任选）。

## 二、重点词汇回顾

- 爱好：collect, stamp, hobby, pleasure, knowledge, skill
- 夏令营：fill out, form, native speaker, improve, look forward to
- 假期：camp, tent, frightened, in the distance, go for a walk
- 友谊：quarrel, mention, forgive, apologize, keep a secret
- 媒体：studio, programme, interview, host, audience, on air

高频短语：tidy up / as well / remember to do / arrive in / take it easy / face to face / get on well with / show sb. around / come true

## 三、核心语法梳理

**宾语从句"三查"**：一查引导词（that / if / whether / 疑问词），二查语序（必须陈述语序），三查时态（主过从过；客观真理用现在时）。

- I hear **(that)** he has gone abroad.
- She asked **whether** I had finished the form.
- Do you know **when the show will begin**?

**过去进行时 + when/while**：I **was reading when** the light went out.

**双宾语**：to 类（give, send, show, lend）/ for 类（buy, make, cook）。

**祈使句 + and/or**：Hurry up, **and** you'll catch the bus. / Be quiet, **or** you'll wake the baby.

## 四、易错点集锦

1. ❌ Do you know where **does she live**? → ✅ where **she lives**
2. ❌ He said he **will** help me. → ✅ he **would** help me
3. ❌ Give **me it**. → ✅ Give **it to me**.
4. ❌ While I **got** home, it was raining. → ✅ **When** I got home...
5. ❌ What a bad weather! → ✅ What bad weather!

## 五、综合练习

1. 单项选择：— Could you tell me ______? — Next Friday.（A. when will the camp start  B. when the camp will start  C. when did the camp start）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - My grandpa was sleeping while I ______ (do) my homework.
   - ______ (collect) coins has been his hobby since 2020.
3. 句型转换：He bought a gift for his mother.（改双宾语语序）→ He bought ______ ______ a gift.
4. 话题作文（提纲）：My Best Friend——如何相识（过去时）；相处多久（现在完成时）；一次小矛盾及和解（宾语从句：I realized that...）；对友谊的看法。

**答案**：1. B  2. was doing; Collecting  3. his mother  4. 略（要求：四段提纲齐全，至少一个宾语从句、一处现在完成时）
