# Unit 2 Hobbies can make you grow as a person

标签：#Module6 #阅读课 #爱好与成长 #议论文

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 6 的阅读课，一篇谈"爱好使人成长"（Hobbies can make you grow as a person）的文章。文中以美国学生 David Smith 为例：他在暑期营学会了写作，之后出版了自己的书，写作这一爱好给他带来了成功与快乐。文章指出爱好不仅带来乐趣，还能帮我们学知识、长本领。

阅读时抓住议论文的结构：观点（爱好让人成长）→ 例证（David 的写作经历）→ 总结（爱好的意义）。注意文中 not only ... but also、as a result 等衔接词的作用。

关键句：
- Hobbies **can make you grow** as a person. 爱好能使你成长。
- Many hobbies **bring us** pleasure and interest. 许多爱好带给我们快乐和兴趣。
- He **has written** many books **since then**. 从那以后他写了很多书。

## 二、重点词汇

- **hobby** n. 爱好（pl. hobbies）  **interest** n. 兴趣
- **pleasure** n. 快乐  **knowledge** n. 知识
- **skill** n. 技能  **success** n. 成功
- **result** n. 结果  **camp** n. 营地
- **develop** v. 发展；培养  **come out** 出版
- **as a result** 结果  **be interested in** 对……感兴趣
- **such as** 例如  **at the same time** 同时

## 三、语法要点

**make + 宾语 + 动词原形/形容词**

- Hobbies can **make you grow**.（make sb. do sth.）
- Reading **makes me happy**.（make sb. + adj.）

**动名词作主语**

- **Writing** brought him pleasure and success.
- **Collecting stamps** is a popular hobby.（动名词作主语，谓语用单数）

**since then + 现在完成时**：He has written many books **since then**.

## 四、易错点

1. knowledge、pleasure 一般不可数：much knowledge（❌ knowledges）
2. 动名词主语接单数谓语：Swimming **is** fun.（❌ are）
3. ❌ make sb. **to do** → ✅ make sb. **do**（使役动词省 to）

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：______ model planes ______ one of his hobbies.（A. Make; is  B. Making; is  C. Making; are）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - His first book came out and it brought him great ______ (succeed).
   - Hobbies can help us develop many ______ (skill).
3. 同义句转换：He became a successful writer because of his hobby. = His hobby made him ______ a successful writer.
4. 汉译英：爱好不仅带给我们快乐，还能帮助我们学到很多知识。

**答案**：1. B  2. success; skills  3. become  4. Hobbies not only bring us pleasure but also help us learn a lot of knowledge.
