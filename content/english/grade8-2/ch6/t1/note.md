# Unit 1 Do you collect anything?

标签：#Module6 #爱好 #听说课 #双宾语

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 6 Hobbies 的听说课。玲玲和大明谈论收藏爱好（Do you collect anything?）：玲玲收集娃娃，大明收集邮票，还提到别人送给他们的礼物。对话围绕"收藏什么、收藏了多久、谁送的"展开，自然复现现在完成时，并引入**双宾语结构 give sb. sth.**。

听对话抓三个问题：What do they collect? How long have they collected them? Who gave them the things? 注意 some 与 any 在肯定句、疑问句中的使用。

关键句：
- **Do you collect anything?** 你收藏什么东西吗？
- My aunt **gave me** a doll from Japan. 我姑姑给了我一个日本娃娃。
- I **have collected** stamps **for five years**. 我集邮五年了。

## 二、重点词汇

- **collect** v. 收集  **collection** n. 收藏品
- **stamp** n. 邮票  **doll** n. 玩具娃娃
- **coin** n. 硬币  **model plane** 飞机模型
- **tidy** adj. 整洁的 v. 收拾  **untidy** adj. 凌乱的
- **as well** 也（句末）  **all the time** 一直
- **take up space** 占空间  **tidy up** 收拾整齐

## 三、语法要点

**双宾语结构**

- give / send / show / pass / lend + **sb. sth.**
  = give / send / show / pass / lend + **sth. to sb.**
- My friend **gave me a stamp**. = My friend **gave a stamp to me**.
- buy / make / cook + sb. sth. = buy sth. **for** sb.：Mum **bought me** a gift. = Mum bought a gift **for me**.

**some / any**：some 用于肯定句和表建议的疑问句；any 用于否定句、一般疑问句。
- Would you like **some** tea?（建议）/ Do you collect **any**thing?

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ give **to me** a book → ✅ give me a book / give a book to me
2. 代词作直接宾语时只能用介词式：Give **it to me**.（❌ Give me it.）
3. as well 放句末，不用逗号：I collect coins **as well**.

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Please pass ______, Tom.（A. me it  B. it to me  C. it me）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - My uncle sent a model plane ______ (介词) me last week.
   - Her room is full of dolls, so it looks ______ (tidy) sometimes.
3. 同义句转换：My grandma made me a kite. = My grandma made a kite ______ ______.
4. 汉译英：你收藏什么东西吗？——是的，我集邮已经三年了。

**答案**：1. B  2. to; untidy  3. for me  4. Do you collect anything? — Yes, I have collected stamps for three years.
