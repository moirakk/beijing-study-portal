# Unit 1 I've also entered lots of speaking competitions

标签：#Module2 #现在完成时 #听说课 #经历

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 2 Experiences 的听说课。对话中 Lingling 和大明谈论各自的经历：Lingling 参加过多次英语演讲比赛（I've also entered lots of speaking competitions），大明则问她是否赢过奖。对话首次系统呈现**现在完成时**谈论"到目前为止的经历"。

听对话时抓三个信号词：**ever**（曾经，用于疑问句）、**never**（从未，用于否定）、**already / just**（已经/刚刚）。回答 Have you ever...? 用 Yes, I have. / No, I haven't（或 No, never）。

关键句：
- **Have you ever entered** a competition? 你参加过比赛吗？
- I**'ve won** several prizes. 我赢过好几个奖。
- I**'ve never travelled** abroad. 我从未出过国。

## 二、重点词汇

- **competition** n. 比赛  **prize** n. 奖；奖品
- **enter** v. 参加；进入  **take part in** 参加（活动）
- **abroad** adv. 在国外  **hometown** n. 家乡
- **ever** adv. 曾经  **never** adv. 从未
- **win a prize** 获奖  **speak to** 与……交谈
- **more than** 超过  **all over the world** 全世界

## 三、语法要点

**现在完成时（一）：谈论经历**

- 构成：have / has + **过去分词**
- 用法：表示"从过去到现在为止"的经历，常与 ever, never 连用。
- 肯定：I **have visited** Beijing twice.
- 否定：She **has never eaten** sushi.
- 疑问：**Have** you **ever ridden** a horse? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

**规则过去分词**：play → played；live → lived；stop → stopped
**常见不规则**：see → seen；win → won；eat → eaten；go → gone；be → been

## 四、易错点

1. 三单用 has：He **has**（❌ have）been to Shanghai.
2. ❌ Have you ever **went**...? → ✅ Have you ever **gone / been**...?（用过去分词）
3. never 位置：放在 have/has 之后：I have **never** seen it.

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：— ______ you ever ______ a speech competition? — Yes, I have.（A. Did; enter  B. Have; entered  C. Do; enter）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - My brother has ______ (win) first prize in the game.
   - I have never ______ (be) abroad.
3. 改为否定句：She has visited the Great Wall. → She ______ ______ the Great Wall.
4. 汉译英：我参加过很多英语比赛，并且赢过两次奖。

**答案**：1. B  2. won; been  3. hasn't visited  4. I have entered / taken part in many English competitions and (I have) won prizes twice.
