# Unit 1 Could I ask if you've mentioned this to her?

标签：#Module9 #友谊 #听说课 #宾语从句

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 9 Friendship 的听说课。玲玲向老师倾诉与好朋友闹矛盾的烦恼，老师耐心询问：我能问问你是否已经跟她提过这件事了吗（Could I ask if you've mentioned this to her?），并建议她主动沟通、当面解释。

对话呈现 **if/whether 引导的宾语从句**（表示"是否"）以及委婉提问句式 Could I ask...?。听时理清"矛盾起因 — 老师的提问 — 建议"三条线索。

关键句：
- **Could I ask if** you've mentioned this **to** her? 我能问问你是否跟她提过这事吗？
- I don't know **whether she will listen** to me. 我不知道她是否会听我说。
- **Why don't you** talk to her face to face? 为什么不和她当面谈谈呢？

## 二、重点词汇

- **mention** v. 提到  **bottom** n. 底部
- **quarrel** n./v. 争吵  **matter** n. 事情；问题
- **silly** adj. 愚蠢的  **patient** adj. 耐心的
- **at the bottom of** 在……底部  **say sorry to** 向……道歉
- **have a talk with** 与……谈一谈  **face to face** 面对面
- **refuse to do** 拒绝做  **make friends again** 重新和好

## 三、语法要点

**if / whether 引导的宾语从句**

- 由一般疑问句变来，表示"是否"，从句用**陈述语序**：
- Do you know...? + Has she agreed? → Do you know **if she has agreed**?
- I wonder **whether he will come** (or not).

**注意**：
1. or not 连用时只能用 whether：whether or not。
2. 从句时态：主句现在时，从句按需要；主句过去时，从句用过去范畴时态。

**委婉请求**：Could I ask...? / Would you mind...?

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ I don't know if **has she** told him. → ✅ if **she has** told him（陈述语序）
2. ❌ if or not → ✅ **whether** or not
3. quarrel with sb. about sth.：They quarrelled **with** each other **about** the game.

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Could you tell me ______ she is coming to the party or not?（A. if  B. whether  C. that）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - I wonder if she ______ (mention) this to you yet.
   - He refused ______ (say) sorry first.
3. 合并句子：Has Lucy found her book? Do you know? → Do you know ______ Lucy ______ ______ her book?
4. 汉译英：我能问问你是否已经和她当面谈过了吗？

**答案**：1. B  2. has mentioned; to say  3. if/whether; has found  4. Could I ask if/whether you have talked with her face to face?
