# Unit 1 I can hardly believe we're in the city centre

标签：#Module8 #听说课 #城市游览 #hardly

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 8 Time off 的听说课。玲玲和朋友在假期游览城市公园，惊叹于市中心竟有如此安静美丽的地方：真不敢相信我们在市中心（I can hardly believe we're in the city centre）。对话描写公园里的湖水、小山、树木，练习用形容词和感叹句表达观感。

听对话时注意 **hardly**（几乎不）这一半否定副词的用法，以及 so...that、such...that 表达程度的句式。

关键句：
- I **can hardly believe** (that) we're in the city centre. 我几乎不敢相信我们在市中心。
- It's **so quiet** — no noise at all. 这里如此安静——一点噪音都没有。
- **What a** beautiful lake! 多美的湖啊！

## 二、重点词汇

- **hardly** adv. 几乎不  **nature** n. 自然
- **noise** n. 噪音  **view** n. 景色
- **lake** n. 湖  **hill** n. 小山
- **wide** adj. 宽阔的  **peaceful** adj. 宁静的
- **time off** 休假  **at all**（否定句中）根本
- **go boating** 去划船  **take it easy** 放轻松
- **be surprised at** 对……感到惊讶

## 三、语法要点

**hardly 的用法**

- hardly = almost not，本身含否定意义，句中不再加 not。
- I can **hardly** hear you. 我几乎听不见你说话。
- 反意疑问句用肯定：He hardly works, **does he**?

**so / such ... that ...（如此……以至于……）**

- so + 形容词/副词 + that：The park is **so quiet that** I can hardly believe it.
- such + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词 + that：It's **such a beautiful park that** everyone loves it.

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ I can't hardly believe it. → ✅ I can **hardly** believe it.（hardly 不与 not 连用）
2. hardly 与 hard 区别：work **hard**（努力）/ **hardly** work（几乎不工作）
3. so + adj. + a/an + 名词 = such + a/an + adj. + 名词：**so** beautiful **a** park = **such a** beautiful park

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：It was ______ quiet in the park ______ we could hear the birds clearly.（A. such; that  B. so; that  C. very; that）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - I could ______ (hard) believe my eyes when I saw the view.
   - What ______ (a) wide lake it is!
3. 同义句转换：It is such an interesting place that I want to visit it again. = The place is ______ ______ that I want to visit it again.
4. 汉译英：市中心如此安静，我几乎不敢相信。

**答案**：1. B  2. hardly; a  3. so interesting  4. It's so quiet in the city centre that I can hardly believe it.
