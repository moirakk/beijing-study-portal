# Unit 3 Language in use

标签：#Module8 #语法总结 #过去进行时 #when/while

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 8 的语法归纳课，系统总结**过去进行时**的构成与用法、when 与 while 的区别，以及 so/such...that 结构。综合任务是写一篇假期旅行见闻的记叙文。

记叙文写作模板：
1. 开头：时间地点人物（Last summer, my family went to...）。
2. 中间：见闻与突发事件（While we were..., ... suddenly...）。
3. 结尾：感受（What a ... trip! / I can hardly forget it.）。

时态搭配示范：
- The sun **was shining** when we **arrived**. 我们到达时阳光正好。
- While I **was taking** photos, my brother **fell** into the water! 我拍照时弟弟掉水里了！

## 二、重点词汇

- **shine** v. 照耀（过去式 shone）  **sunshine** n. 阳光
- **beach** n. 海滩  **island** n. 岛
- **pleasant** adj. 令人愉快的  **exciting** adj. 刺激的
- **have a picnic** 野餐  **go for a walk** 去散步
- **fall asleep** 入睡  **at that moment** 在那时
- **can't wait to do** 迫不及待做

## 三、语法要点

**过去进行时 vs. 一般过去时**

| 时态 | 用法 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 过去进行时 | 过去某刻正在进行（背景） | I **was reading** at 8 p.m. |
| 一般过去时 | 过去发生并结束（事件） | The phone **rang** at 8 p.m. |

**when / while 使用要点**

1. when + 短暂动作；while + 延续动作。
2. 两个动作同时进行用 while：**While** mum was cooking, dad was cleaning.
3. was/were doing... when...（正在……突然……）：I **was walking** home **when** it began to rain.

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ I **was knowing** the answer. → ✅ I **knew** the answer.（状态动词无进行时）
2. 主语单复数：I/he/she **was** doing；we/you/they **were** doing
3. ❌ While the phone rang... → ✅ **When** the phone rang...（短暂动作用 when）

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：They ______ a picnic when it suddenly started to rain.（A. had  B. were having  C. have）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - The sun ______ (shine) brightly while we were walking on the beach.
   - I fell asleep when I ______ (watch) TV last night.
3. 改错：While I opened the door, the cat ran out. ______
4. 书面表达（4句）：写你假期中的一个片段，须含一个"was/were doing... when..."句。

**答案**：1. B  2. was shining; was watching  3. While → When  4. 示例：Last Sunday I went to the park. I was flying a kite when an old friend called my name. We were both surprised. What a pleasant day it was!
