# Unit 2 We have played football for a year now

标签：#Module4 #阅读课 #健康生活 #for/since

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 4 的阅读课。课文讲述"我"的健康转变故事：过去沉迷电脑、缺乏运动、身体越来越差；自从加入学校足球队，已经踢了一年球（We have played football for a year now），身体变得健康强壮，学习也更有精力了。

阅读时按"过去的不良习惯 → 改变的契机 → 现在的状态"梳理时间线，体会文中一般过去时（过去的习惯）与现在完成时（延续到现在的改变）的交替使用。文章结尾点明主旨：运动和健康饮食让生活更美好。

关键句：
- I **used to spend** hours playing computer games. 我过去常花几个小时玩电脑游戏。
- We **have played** football **for a year** now. 到现在我们踢足球已经一年了。
- I**'m** much **healthier and happier** than before. 我比以前健康快乐多了。

## 二、重点词汇

- **weak** adj. 虚弱的  **strong** adj. 强壮的
- **exercise** n./v. 锻炼  **habit** n. 习惯
- **member** n. 成员  **train** v. 训练
- **condition** n. 状况  **awful** adj. 糟糕的
- **take up** 开始从事  **be crazy about** 痴迷于
- **all day long** 一整天  **in good/poor health** 健康状况好/差
- **make a difference** 产生影响

## 三、语法要点

**瞬间动词与延续性动词的转换**（现在完成时 + 时间段）

| 瞬间动词 | 延续替换 |
| --- | --- |
| join | be in / be a member of |
| buy | have |
| die | be dead |
| come/arrive | be here |

- He **joined** the team a year ago. = He **has been in** the team **for a year**.

**比较级 + than before**：I feel **stronger than before**.

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ He has joined the club for two years. → ✅ He has **been in** the club for two years.
2. exercise 作"锻炼"不可数：do **much exercise**；作"练习题"可数：do exercises
3. used to do 表过去习惯（现在已不再）：I **used to be** weak.

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：My uncle ______ this car since 2022.（A. bought  B. has bought  C. has had）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - Doing sports every day makes me ______ (healthy) than before.
   - He has been a ______ (member) of the football team for a year.
3. 同义句转换：Lucy joined the reading club two months ago. = Lucy has ______ ______ the reading club for two months.
4. 汉译英：自从开始锻炼，我的健康状况产生了很大变化。

**答案**：1. C  2. healthier; member  3. been in  4. Since I took up / started exercising, my health has made a big difference. (或 There has been a big change in my health since I started to exercise.)
