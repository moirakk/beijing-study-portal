# Unit 3 Language in use

标签：#Module4 #语法总结 #for/since #看病用语

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 4 的语法归纳课，全面总结现在完成时与 **for / since** 连用的用法，并复习看病场景的功能用语。课堂活动包括：for/since 分类填空、根据病历卡编看病对话、写一段自己的健康生活计划。

学完本单元要能：熟练区分 for + 时间段与 since + 时间点；掌握瞬间动词转延续性动词的四组常考替换；流利完成一段医生与病人的对话。

看病功能句归纳：
- 医生：What's the matter (with you)? / How long have you been like this? / Take this medicine three times a day.
- 病人：I've got a cough. / Since Tuesday. / How often should I take it?

## 二、重点词汇

- **temperature** n. 体温  **result** n. 结果
- **anything serious** 严重的事  **rest** n./v. 休息
- **three times a day** 一天三次  **stay in bed** 卧床
- **plenty of** 大量的  **be harmful to** 对……有害
- **give up** 放弃  **from now on** 从现在起
- **as soon as possible** 尽快

## 三、语法要点

**for / since 判断技巧**

- for + **一段时间**：for two hours / for half a year / for a long time
- since + **一个时间点**：since 8 o'clock / since last month / since 2023
- since + **一般过去时从句**：since he was a child

**How long / When 提问的区别**

- **How long** have you lived here? — For ten years.（问时长，用完成时）
- **When** did you move here? — Ten years ago.（问时间点，用过去时）

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ since two days → ✅ **for** two days（两天是时间段）
2. ❌ How long did you **come** here? → ✅ **When** did you come here?（come 瞬间动词不与 how long 连用）
3. plenty of 接可数或不可数名词均可：plenty of water / plenty of vegetables

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：He has taught in this school ______ he was twenty-five.（A. for  B. since  C. when）
2. 用 for 或 since 填空：
   - I have known her ______ ten years.
   - They have lived in Beijing ______ 2015.
3. 对画线部分提问：She has been ill for a week. → ______ ______ has she been ill?
4. 情景对话排序：a. Take this medicine and rest more. b. What's the matter? c. I've got a headache. d. How long have you been like this? e. Since yesterday.

**答案**：1. B  2. for; since  3. How long  4. b → c → d → e → a
