# Unit 1 I haven't done much exercise since I got my computer

标签：#Module4 #现在完成时 #for/since #健康

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 4 Seeing the doctor 的听说课。大明去看医生，描述自己的症状：头疼、胃疼。医生询问病史后发现原因——自从有了电脑，他就没怎么运动（I haven't done much exercise since I got my computer），还经常吃快餐。医生建议他多运动、健康饮食。

对话呈现现在完成时与 **since / for** 连用表示"状态或动作延续到现在"。看病场景的功能句也是重点：What's the matter? / How long have you been like this? / You should...

关键句：
- I**'ve got** a headache and a stomach ache. 我头疼、胃疼。
- **How long have** you **been** like this? 你这样多久了？
- **Since** Friday. / **For** three days. 从周五开始。/ 三天了。

## 二、重点词汇

- **headache** n. 头疼  **stomach ache** 胃疼
- **cold** n. 感冒  **fever** n. 发烧
- **cough** n./v. 咳嗽  **medicine** n. 药
- **ill** adj. 生病的  **health** n. 健康
- **fast food** 快餐  **take sb.'s temperature** 量体温
- **catch a cold** 感冒  **take medicine** 吃药
- **since** prep./conj. 自从  **for** prep. 达……之久

## 三、语法要点

**现在完成时（三）：for 与 since**

- **for + 时间段**：I have been ill **for two days**.
- **since + 时间点/过去时从句**：
  - He has lived here **since 2020**.
  - I haven't played football **since I got my computer**.（从句用一般过去时）

**How long...? 提问延续时间**：
- **How long** have you had this bike? — For a year. / Since last year.

**注意**：延续性动词（live, have, be, keep）才能与 for/since 连用。

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ I have bought this bike **for a year**. → ✅ I have **had** this bike for a year.（buy 是瞬间动词，改用 have）
2. since 从句用一般过去时：since I **came**（❌ since I have come）
3. ill 作表语，sick 可作定语：a **sick** boy / He is **ill**.

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：— How long have you been like this? — ______ last night.（A. For  B. Since  C. From）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - I haven't seen him since he ______ (go) to Beijing.
   - She has been a nurse ______ (for / since) ten years.
3. 改错：He has borrowed the book for two weeks. ______
4. 汉译英：自从上周五以来，我一直感冒发烧。

**答案**：1. B  2. went; for  3. borrowed → kept  4. I have had a cold and a fever since last Friday.
