# Unit 2 Tintin has been popular for over eighty years

标签：#Module5 #阅读课 #经典卡通 #现在完成时综合

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 5 的阅读课，介绍中外经典卡通形象：比利时漫画《丁丁历险记》的主角丁丁已经风靡八十多年（Tintin has been popular for over eighty years）；美国的史努比（Snoopy）以及中国的美猴王等也深受几代人喜爱。文章分析这些卡通形象长盛不衰的原因——有趣的故事、鲜明的性格。

阅读时用表格整理：卡通形象 — 来自哪国 — 诞生时间 — 特点。注意文中"has been popular for + 时间段"这一现在完成时结构，把 Module 2-4 学的完成时用法在语篇中综合起来。

关键句：
- Tintin **has been popular for** over eighty years. 丁丁已经风靡八十多年。
- Tintin's stories **have been translated into** many languages. 丁丁的故事被译成多种语言。
- Snoopy **first appeared** in 1950. 史努比于 1950 年首次亮相。

## 二、重点词汇

- **popular** adj. 受欢迎的  **private** adj. 私人的
- **detective** n. 侦探  **artist** n. 艺术家；画家
- **appear** v. 出现  **create** v. 创作
- **satisfy** v. 使满意  **lovely** adj. 可爱的
- **at the age of** 在……岁时  **over** prep. 超过（= more than）
- **be popular with** 受……欢迎  **not only ... but also ...** 不但……而且……

## 三、语法要点

**be popular + 现在完成时**（状态延续）

- The cartoon **has been popular for** 80 years.（be 是延续性动词，可接 for/since）
- 比较：The cartoon **became popular** in 1929.（became 瞬间，接过去时间点）

**not only ... but also ... 连接并列成分**

- He is **not only** smart **but also** brave.
- 连接主语时谓语就近一致：Not only the children but also the mother **likes** Snoopy.

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ Tintin has become popular for 80 years. → ✅ has **been** popular（become 瞬间动词）
2. appear 不及物，无被动：The hero **appeared** in 1950.（❌ was appeared）
3. at the age of + 数字 = when sb. was + 数字：**at the age of** ten

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Mickey Mouse ______ popular for almost a hundred years.（A. has become  B. has been  C. became）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - The artist ______ (create) Snoopy in 1950.
   - Tintin is popular ______ (介词) readers all over the world.
3. 同义句转换：He began to draw cartoons when he was eight. = He began to draw cartoons ______ ______ ______ ______ eight.
4. 汉译英：这部动画片不但有趣，而且教会我们要勇敢。

**答案**：1. B  2. created; with  3. at the age of  4. This cartoon is not only interesting but also teaches us to be brave.
