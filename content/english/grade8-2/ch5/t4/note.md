# Revision module A

标签：#复习模块A #Module1-5 #期中复习 #现在完成时

## 一、复习范围

Revision module A 综合复习 Module 1-5 的全部核心内容，是期中考试前的系统梳理：

1. **Module 1** 感官系动词 + 形容词（look / sound / smell / taste / feel）
2. **Module 2** 现在完成时表经历（ever / never；have been to vs. have gone to）
3. **Module 3** 现在完成时 + just / already / yet
4. **Module 4** 现在完成时 + for / since；看病用语
5. **Module 5** 现在完成时综合运用；介绍卡通人物

复习方法：先默写各模块不规则动词过去分词，再做句式转换专项，最后限时完成一篇综合阅读和一篇话题作文。

## 二、重点词汇回顾

- 感官与情感：nervous, proud, lonely, delicious, fresh, sour
- 经历与旅行：competition, prize, abroad, pyramid, experience
- 太空：spacecraft, astronaut, planet, universe, so far
- 健康：headache, fever, cough, medicine, take exercise
- 卡通：hero, magic, humorous, appear, be popular with

高频短语：try on / be proud of / take part in / come true / catch a cold / give up / come out / not only ... but also ...

## 三、核心语法梳理

**现在完成时一张表**

- 构成：have/has + 过去分词
- 经历：I have **never** been abroad.
- 完成：She has **already** left. / Has he arrived **yet**?
- 延续：We have known each other **for** ten years / **since** 2016.

**易混对比**：
1. have been to（去过）vs. have gone to（去了未归）
2. 现在完成时（无具体过去时间）vs. 一般过去时（yesterday, ago）
3. 瞬间动词接时间段要转换：buy → have；join → be in；die → be dead

**系表结构**：The soup tastes **good**.（形容词作表语）

## 四、易错点集锦

1. ❌ I have finished it yesterday. → ✅ I finished it yesterday.
2. ❌ The flowers smell well. → ✅ smell sweet/nice.
3. ❌ He has died for two years. → ✅ He has been dead for two years.
4. ❌ Why not to have a rest? → ✅ Why not have a rest?

## 五、综合练习

1. 单项选择：— Have you ______ been to the Science Museum? — Yes, I went there last month.（A. never  B. ever  C. yet）
2. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - The cake smells ______ (wonderful), and it tastes even ______ (good).
   - My father ______ (be) to Egypt twice. He ______ (go) there first in 2018.
3. 句型转换：He has already handed in his homework.（改否定句）→ He ______ handed in his homework ______.
4. 话题作文（提纲）：My Healthy Life——过去的坏习惯（used to）；现在的改变（现在完成时 + for/since）；感受与打算。

**答案**：1. B  2. wonderful, better; has been, went  3. hasn't; yet  4. 略（要求：三段式，至少两处现在完成时）
