# Unit 2·重点梳理

标签：#Unit2 #语法梳理 #不规则动词 #过去时叙事

## 一、本单元语法总览

Unit 2 的语法核心：**一般过去时的不规则动词**，并综合运用过去时讲述一段完整经历。与 Unit 1 的规则动词合在一起，构成"过去时"完整拼图。

## 二、必背不规则动词表（七下高频）

| 原形 | 过去式 | 原形 | 过去式 |
|---|---|---|---|
| go | went | come | came |
| see | saw | take | took |
| make | made | get | got |
| have | had | do | did |
| say | said | find | found |
| feel | felt | think | thought |
| run | ran | swim | swam |
| win | won | fall | fell |
| begin | began | eat | ate |
| give | gave | know | knew |
| ride | rode | write | wrote |
| buy | bought | teach | taught |

**记忆技巧**：按变化类型分组记——变元音（swim-swam）、变 -ought/-aught（buy-bought, teach-taught）、完全不变（put-put, read-read）。

## 三、过去时叙事的"时间线"表达

| 表达 | 用法 |
|---|---|
| at first | 起初（引出开头的困难/紧张） |
| then / next / after that | 接着（推进过程） |
| finally / in the end / at last | 最后（给出结果） |
| for the first time | 第一次（常放句尾） |

例：**At first** I was afraid of the water. **Then** my coach taught me step by step. **Finally**, I swam across the pool!

## 四、重点句型结构

1. **It took sb + 时间 + to do sth**：It took me a month to learn skating.
2. **be afraid of + n./doing**：He was afraid of falling.
3. **succeed in doing / fail to do**：She succeeded in passing the test.
4. **encourage sb to do**：My teacher encouraged me to have a try.

## 五、易错点提醒

- 不规则动词加 -ed 是零分操作：taked ❌ took ✔；runned ❌ ran ✔。
- read 的过去式拼写不变但读音变 /red/。
- did 出现后动词必须原形，包括特殊疑问句：How did you felt ❌ → How did you feel ✔。
- win 的宾语是比赛/奖品（win the game），"赢某人"用 beat sb。

## 六、小练习

1. I ______ (fall) off my bike but I got up and rode on.
2. She ______ (think) the problem was too hard at first.
3. — What ______ you ______ (eat) for breakfast this morning? — An egg and some bread.
4. It ______ (take) him three days to finish the storybook last week.
5. 改错：Our class winned the basketball match yesterday.

**答案**：1. fell 2. thought 3. did; eat 4. took 5. winned → won
