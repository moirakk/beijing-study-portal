# Unit 3·综合练习

标签：#Unit3 #单元测试 #综合练习

## 一、练习说明

覆盖 Unit 3 核心：饮食词汇、可数/不可数名词、量词短语、some/any、祈使句与系动词。建议限时 25 分钟。

## 二、单项选择

1. — Would you like ______ bread? — Yes, just a little, please.
   A. any B. some C. many
2. There ______ some chicken and two apples on the table.
   A. is B. are C. am
3. We need three ______ for this dish.
   A. tomato B. tomatoes C. tomatos
4. — How ______ rice do you want? — A bowl, please.
   A. many B. much C. often
5. The cake smells ______. I can't wait to eat it!
   A. well B. good C. goodly
6. ______ the eggs with the flour, and then heat the pan.
   A. Mix B. Mixing C. To mix
7. Mum bought two ______ this morning.
   A. kilo of meat B. kilos of meat C. kilos of meats
8. ______ eat too much ice cream. It's bad for you.
   A. Not B. Don't C. No

## 三、用所给词的适当形式填空

1. Could I have three ______ (glass) of water?
2. The vegetables ______ (be) fresh and cheap in this market.
3. ______ (cut) the beef into small pieces first.
4. There isn't ______ (much/many) juice left in the fridge.
5. This dish ______ (taste) a little spicy but very delicious.

## 四、按要求改写句子

1. We need some eggs for the recipe.（改为一般疑问句）→ ______
2. Add some salt to the soup.（改为否定句）→ ______
3. I'd like two bowls of noodles.（对画线部分提问，画线：two bowls of）→ ______
4. into, the, pieces, apple, cut（连词成句）→ ______

## 五、汉译英

1. 首先把西红柿切成小块。
2. 你想要一些牛奶吗？
3. 这道菜闻起来很香，尝起来更好。
4. 别往汤里加太多盐。

## 六、参考答案

**二、单项选择**
1. B（Would you like 句型用 some）2. A（就近原则，chicken 不可数）3. B（tomato 复数加 es）4. B（rice 不可数）5. B（smell 后接形容词）6. A（祈使句动词原形开头）7. B（量词 kilo 变复数，meat 不变）8. B（否定祈使句 Don't）

**三、词形填空**
1. glasses 2. are 3. Cut 4. much 5. tastes

**四、改写句子**
1. Do we need any eggs for the recipe?（some 变 any）
2. Don't add any salt to the soup.
3. How many bowls of noodles would you like?
4. Cut the apple into pieces.

**五、汉译英**
1. First, cut the tomatoes into small pieces.
2. Would you like some milk?
3. This dish smells good and tastes even better.
4. Don't put too much salt in the soup.

## 七、易错提醒

- 第 2 题：there be 就近原则——离 be 最近的名词决定单复数。
- 第 7 题：量词复数变化在 kilo 上，meat 永远不加 s。
- 改写句子第 1 题：肯定句变疑问句时 some 通常改为 any。
