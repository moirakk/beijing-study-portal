# Unit 1·重点梳理

标签：#Unit1 #语法梳理 #一般过去时 #be动词过去式

## 一、本单元语法总览

Unit 1 的语法核心：**一般过去时**——包括 be 动词的过去式（was/were）和实义动词的规则变化。这是七年级下学期的第一大语法关卡，也是后面单元讲故事、写游记的基础。

## 二、be 动词的过去式

| 主语 | 现在式 | 过去式 |
|---|---|---|
| I / he / she / it / 单数名词 | am / is | **was** |
| you / we / they / 复数名词 | are | **were** |

- 否定：was not = **wasn't**；were not = **weren't**。
- 疑问：Was/Were 提到句首。Were you at home last night? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.

## 三、规则动词过去式的四条变化规则

| 规则 | 例词 |
|---|---|
| 一般直接加 -ed | help → helped, play → played |
| 以不发音 e 结尾加 -d | smile → smiled, live → lived |
| 辅音字母 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -ed | study → studied, carry → carried |
| 重读闭音节结尾，双写末尾辅音字母加 -ed | stop → stopped, plan → planned |

-ed 的读音：清辅音后读 /t/（helped），浊辅音和元音后读 /d/（played），/t/ /d/ 后读 /ɪd/（wanted, needed）。

## 四、一般过去时的句式变换

以 She visited the museum. 为例：

- 否定句：She **didn't visit** the museum.（did 后还原动词原形）
- 一般疑问句：**Did** she **visit** the museum? — Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.
- 特殊疑问句：**What did** she **do** yesterday?

**口诀**：见 did 用原形，was/were 自己变。

## 五、常考时间标志词

yesterday（昨天）、last night / week / month / year（昨晚/上周/上月/去年）、... ago（……以前）、just now（刚才）、in 2020（在过去某年）、this morning（若已过去）。

## 六、易错点提醒

- **did + 动词原形**：Did you went ❌ → Did you go ✔。
- 否定句忘记还原：She didn't smiled ❌ → She didn't smile ✔。
- be 动词与行为动词不能叠用：I was helped my mum ❌ → I helped my mum ✔（"我帮妈妈"）。
- last night 前不加 in/on/at：I watched TV last night.

## 七、小练习

1. There ______ (be) a football match on TV last night.
2. The children ______ (study) in the library yesterday afternoon.
3. He ______ (not play) computer games last weekend.
4. — ______ your parents at work this morning? — Yes, they were.
5. 改错：Did you visited the Great Wall last year?

**答案**：1. was 2. studied 3. didn't play 4. Were 5. visited → visit
