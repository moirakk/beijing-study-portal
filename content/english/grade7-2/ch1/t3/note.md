# Unit 1·综合练习

标签：#Unit1 #单元测试 #综合练习

## 一、练习说明

覆盖 Unit 1 核心：幸福话题词汇、be 动词过去式、规则动词一般过去时及其句式变换。建议限时 25 分钟。

## 二、单项选择

1. — What made you happy yesterday? — I ______ my grandparents and we had a good time.
   A. visit B. visited C. visits
2. There ______ many people in the park last Sunday.
   A. was B. were C. are
3. Doing simple things every day can bring us ______.
   A. happy B. happiness C. happily
4. — ______ you at school just now? — No, I was at home.
   A. Were B. Did C. Are
5. She didn't ______ TV last night because she was busy.
   A. watched B. watch C. watches
6. My father ______ me a bike two weeks ______.
   A. buyed; ago B. bought; ago C. bought; before
7. Tom smiled ______ me and said "Thank you".
   A. at B. on C. for
8. — Did your sister help you with your homework? — ______
   A. Yes, she was. B. Yes, she did. C. No, she wasn't.

## 三、用所给词的适当形式填空

1. I ______ (be) very tired after the long walk yesterday.
2. We ______ (plan) a birthday party for Mum last weekend.
3. The girl ______ (carry) the heavy box upstairs just now.
4. ______ he ______ (live) in Beijing two years ago?
5. Sharing happiness with others ______ (make) us feel better.

## 四、按要求改写句子

1. They were at the cinema last night.（改为否定句）→ ______
2. She cleaned her room this morning.（改为一般疑问句）→ ______
3. I played football with my friends yesterday.（对画线部分提问，画线：played football）→ ______
4. happy, small, me, things, make（连词成句）→ ______

## 五、汉译英

1. 昨天的足球赛让我们非常开心。
2. 幸福的秘诀之一是帮助别人。
3. 上周末你和家人待在一起了吗？
4. 我昨晚没有玩电脑游戏。

## 六、参考答案

**二、单项选择**
1. B 2. B（people 复数配 were）3. B（bring 后接名词）4. A（just now 也可与 was/were 连用）5. B（didn't 后用原形）6. B（buy 的过去式是 bought；"两周前"用 ago）7. A（smile at 固定搭配）8. B（Did 问 did 答）

**三、词形填空**
1. was 2. planned（双写 n）3. carried（变 y 为 i）4. Did; live 5. makes（动名词短语作主语视为单数）

**四、改写句子**
1. They weren't at the cinema last night.
2. Did she clean her room this morning?
3. What did you do with your friends yesterday?
4. Small things make me happy.

**五、汉译英**
1. The football match yesterday made us very happy.
2. One secret of happiness is to help others.
3. Did you stay with your family last weekend?
4. I didn't play computer games last night.

## 七、易错提醒

- 第 6 题：buy 是不规则动词，过去式 bought；"……以前"用 ago 且置于时间段之后。
- 词形填空第 5 题：Sharing... 作主语是单数概念，谓语用三单，别被复数 others 干扰。
- 改写句子第 2 题：还原 cleaned → clean，did 已承担过去时标志。
