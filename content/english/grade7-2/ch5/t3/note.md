# Unit 5·综合练习

标签：#Unit5 #单元测试 #综合练习

## 一、练习说明

覆盖 Unit 5 核心：自然奇观词汇、形容词比较级构成与句型、比较级修饰语。建议限时 25 分钟。

## 二、单项选择

1. The Dead Sea is much ______ than other seas, so people can float on it easily.
   A. salty B. saltier C. saltiest
2. — Which is ______, the lake or the river? — The lake, I think.
   A. deep B. deeper C. deepest
3. This waterfall is ______ more amazing than the photos show.
   A. very B. even C. quite
4. The weather in the desert is hotter than ______ in the forest.
   A. that B. it C. one
5. Autumn is coming. The days are getting ______.
   A. short and short B. shorter and shorter C. more and more short
6. My hometown is famous ______ its beautiful mountains.
   A. as B. of C. for
7. The strange rock looks ______ an old man.
   A. like B. at C. for
8. — The rainbow ______ after ten minutes. — What a pity! I didn't see it.
   A. appeared B. disappeared C. disappears

## 三、用所给词的适当形式填空

1. The Yellow River is the second ______ (long) river in China.
2. Travelling by train is ______ (cheap) than travelling by plane.
3. Of the two lakes, this one is ______ (big).
4. The higher you climb, the ______ (cold) it gets.
5. I think nothing is ______ (amazing) than nature itself.

## 四、按要求改写句子

1. The river is wide. The lake is wider.（合并为比较级句子）→ ______
2. This mountain is higher than that one.（改为一般疑问句）→ ______
3. Tom is 13. Jack is 15.（用 older 造比较句）→ ______
4. than, deeper, much, the sea, the lake, is（连词成句）→ ______

## 五、汉译英

1. 这个湖比那个湖深得多。
2. 天气越来越暖和了。
3. 哪一个更令人惊叹，瀑布还是盐湖？
4. 这块岩石看起来像一只睡觉的猫。

## 六、参考答案

**二、单项选择**
1. B 2. B（两者比较用比较级）3. B（even 可修饰比较级，very 不行）4. A（that 替代 the weather）5. B（比较级 and 比较级）6. C（be famous for）7. A（look like）8. B（"消失了"才看不到，且用过去时）

**三、词形填空**
1. longest（the second + 最高级）2. cheaper 3. bigger（of the two + the 比较级，此处口语可省 the）4. colder 5. more amazing

**四、改写句子**
1. The lake is wider than the river.
2. Is this mountain higher than that one?
3. Jack is two years older than Tom.
4. The sea is much deeper than the lake.

**五、汉译英**
1. This lake is much deeper than that one.
2. The weather is getting warmer and warmer.
3. Which is more amazing, the waterfall or the salt lake?
4. The rock looks like a sleeping cat.

## 七、易错提醒

- 第 3 题：能修饰比较级的只有 much/even/a little/a lot 等，very/quite/so 都不行。
- 第 4 题：比较对象必须同类，用 that 代替单数不可数、those 代替复数。
- 词形填空第 1 题：the second/third + 最高级表示"第二/第三……的"。
