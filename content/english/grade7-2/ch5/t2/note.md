# Unit 5·重点梳理

标签：#Unit5 #语法梳理 #比较级 #最高级入门

## 一、本单元语法总览

Unit 5 的语法核心：**形容词比较级**（构成、句型、修饰语），并初步接触**最高级**。比较级是七下最重要的语法点之一，期末必考句型转换与单选。

## 二、比较级的构成规则

| 规则 | 例词 |
|---|---|
| 单音节直接加 -er | tall → taller, deep → deeper |
| 以 e 结尾加 -r | wide → wider, nice → nicer |
| 辅音+y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -er | salty → saltier, happy → happier |
| 重读闭音节双写尾字母加 -er | big → bigger, hot → hotter, thin → thinner |
| 多音节/部分双音节用 more | more beautiful, more amazing, more interesting |

**不规则变化必背**：good/well → better；bad/badly → worse；many/much → more；little → less；far → farther/further。

## 三、比较级核心句型

1. **A + be + 比较级 + than + B**：The lake is deeper than the river.
2. **动词句比较**：He runs faster than his brother.
3. **Which/Who is + 比较级, A or B?**：Which is bigger, the sun or the moon?
4. **比较级 + and + 比较级**（越来越……）：The days are getting longer and longer.
5. **The + 比较级..., the + 比较级...**（越……越……）：The more you explore, the more you discover.

## 四、比较级的修饰语

可以放在比较级前的："程度放大镜"**much / even / a lot**（……得多），"程度缩小镜"**a little / a bit**（稍微）。

- The desert is **much hotter** than the forest.
- This mountain is **a little higher** than that one.
- ❌ very / so / too / quite 不能修饰比较级。

## 五、最高级入门（了解）

三者及以上比较用最高级：the + -est / the most + 原级，常配 in/of 短语。

- The Yangtze River is **the longest** river in China.
- Of all the wonders, this one is **the most amazing**.

## 六、易错点提醒

- more taller ❌：-er 与 more 只能二选一。
- than 后同类比较：北京的冬天 vs 上海的冬天 → colder than **that** of Shanghai。
- "A 比 B 大两岁"：A is two years older than B（差值放比较级前）。
- farther 指距离，further 还可指程度（further study 深造）。

## 七、小练习

1. The weather is getting ______ and ______ (warm).
2. My box is even ______ (heavy) than yours.
3. — Which do you like ______ (well), spring or autumn? — Spring.
4. The ______ (much) careful you are, the ______ (few) mistakes you'll make.
5. 改错：This river is very longer than that one.

**答案**：1. warmer; warmer 2. heavier 3. better 4. more; fewer 5. very → much
