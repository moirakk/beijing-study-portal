# Unit 6·重点梳理

标签：#Unit6 #语法梳理 #最高级 #begoingto

## 一、本单元语法总览

Unit 6 的语法核心：**形容词最高级**（与 Unit 5 比较级衔接成完整的"比较体系"）、**be going to 表计划**。这是期末综合复习的重头戏。

## 二、最高级的构成规则

| 规则 | 例词 |
|---|---|
| 单音节加 -est | tall → tallest, long → longest |
| 以 e 结尾加 -st | nice → nicest, wide → widest |
| 辅音+y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -est | happy → happiest, early → earliest |
| 重读闭音节双写尾字母加 -est | big → biggest, hot → hottest |
| 多音节用 the most | the most beautiful, the most unforgettable |

**不规则**：good/well → best；bad → worst；many/much → most；little → least；far → farthest/furthest。

## 三、原级 · 比较级 · 最高级三档对照

| 场景 | 句型 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 两者相同 | as + 原级 + as | This bag is as heavy as that one. |
| 两者比较 | 比较级 + than | The train is faster than the bus. |
| 三者及以上 | the + 最高级 + in/of | The plane is the fastest of the three. |

**同义转换**：Tom is the tallest in his class. = Tom is taller than **any other student** in his class.

## 四、最高级高频句式

1. **the + 最高级 + in/of 范围**：in my class（范围内）/ of all the seasons（同类中）。
2. **one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词**：one of the most famous cities。
3. **the + 序数词 + 最高级**：the second longest river 第二长的河。
4. **疑问**：Which season do you like best? / What's the best way to travel?

## 五、be going to 表计划

- 结构：**am/is/are + going to + 动词原形**，表示事先打算好的计划。
- We are going to take a trip to Yunnan. / She isn't going to travel by plane.
- 疑问：Are you going to pack tonight? — Yes, I am.
- 标志词：next week/month, this summer, tomorrow。
- 注意：go 可以说 be going to go，也常直接说 We are going to Beijing。

## 六、易错点提醒

- 最高级忘 the：Spring is best season ❌ → the best season ✔。
- in/of 混用：the tallest **of** my class ❌ → **in** my class ✔。
- one of the + 最高级后接**复数**名词，但谓语看主语 one（单数）：One of the most popular places **is**...
- be going to 后必须动词原形：is going to visits ❌。

## 七、小练习

1. Winter is ______ (cold) season of the year.
2. Beijing is one of ______ (old) capitals in the world.
3. Bill is ______ (funny) student in our class — he tells the best jokes!
4. — ______ you ______ (visit) your uncle this weekend? — Yes, we are.
5. 同义句转换：The Yangtze River is longer than any other river in China. → The Yangtze River is ______ river in China.

**答案**：1. the coldest 2. the oldest 3. the funniest 4. Are; going to visit 5. the longest
