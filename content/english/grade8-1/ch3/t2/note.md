# Unit 3 Understanding ideas

标签：#Unit3 #UnderstandingIdeas #阅读理解 #未来计划

## 一、课文精讲

Understanding ideas 是 Unit 3 的主阅读板块。课文通常以"如何实现梦想"为主题，讲述某个人物（可能是名人、身边榜样或虚构角色）制定目标、克服困难、最终取得进步的故事。阅读时要关注人物的梦想、具体计划和行动步骤。

阅读策略：先读标题预测人物身份；再读全文找出关键信息：What is his/her dream? What is he/she going to do? Why does he/she want to do it? 注意文中 be going to 的不同用法。

## 二、重点词汇

- **achieve** v. 实现；达到  **success** n. 成功
- **successful** adj. 成功的  **failure** n. 失败
- **difficulty** n. 困难  **challenge** n. 挑战
- **practise** v. 练习  **improve** v. 提高
- **progress** n. 进步  **effort** n. 努力
- **be ready to** 准备好  **give up** 放弃
- **try one's best** 尽某人最大努力  **never give up** 永不放弃
- **in the future** 在将来  **from now on** 从现在开始

## 三、语法要点

**be going to 的多种用法**

1. **表示主观计划或打算**：I'm going to read more books.
2. **表示即将发生的动作（有迹象）**：Look at the clouds. It's going to rain.
3. **表示决心或承诺**：I'm going to finish my homework on time.

**be going to 与 will 的初步区别**

- be going to：事先有计划、有迹象；I'm going to visit my uncle tomorrow.（已计划）
- will：临时决定或客观预测；It's cold. I'll close the window.（临时决定）

## 四、易错点

1. practise 是动词，practice 是名词（英式拼写中动词为 practise）：I **practise** the piano every day.
2. be going to 后接动词原形，不要受 going 影响误加 -ing：❌ *I'm going to playing.* → ✅ I'm going to **play**.
3. dream 作动词时，dream of / about doing sth：He dreams of **becoming** a pilot.

## 五、练习题

1. 用 be going to 或 will 填空：
   - I have decided. I ______ buy a new bike next month.
   - Oh, it's hot in here. I ______ open the window.
2. 单项选择：Lucy practises ______ the violin every afternoon.（A. play  B. playing  C. to play）
3. 句型转换：He is going to be a scientist.（对 a scientist 提问）
4. 汉译英：他从不放弃，并且正尽最大努力提高英语水平。

**答案**：1. am going to; will  2. B  3. What is he going to be?  4. He never gives up and is trying his best to improve his English.
