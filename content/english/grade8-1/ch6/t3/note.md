# Unit 6 Developing ideas

标签：#Unit6 #DevelopingIdeas #whenWhile #安全常识

## 一、课文精讲

Developing ideas 是 Unit 6 的拓展与运用板块。本环节通过安全指南、专家访谈或新闻报道，进一步讨论"灾害来临时如何保护自己"。课文常介绍地震、火灾、洪水等灾害中的自救常识，引导学生用英语表达安全措施和应急步骤。

学习重点是用 when 和 while 引导时间状语从句，结合过去进行时描述灾害发生时的情景；同时学习用祈使句表达安全建议。

## 二、重点词汇

- **safety** n. 安全  **safety tips** 安全提示
- **warning** n. 警告  **alarm** n. 警报
- **exit** n. 出口  **entrance** n. 入口
- **evacuate** v. 疏散  **shelter** n. 避难所
- **first aid** 急救  **emergency kit** 应急包
- **stay calm** 保持冷静  **keep away from** 远离
- **stay indoors** 待在室内  **go outside** 出去
- **turn off** 关闭  **stay away from windows** 远离窗户

## 三、语法要点

**when 与 while 的用法区别**

| 连词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| when | 从句可用一般过去时或过去进行时 | **When** the earthquake happened, I was reading. |
| while | 从句常用过去进行时，强调"在……期间" | **While** I was reading, the earthquake happened. |
| when | 可表示"就在这时"，主句用过去进行时 | I was walking **when** I heard a loud noise. |

**用祈使句表达安全建议**

- **Keep calm** and follow the teacher's instructions.
- **Don't use** the lift during a fire.
- **Stay away from** windows and heavy furniture.

## 四、易错点

1. while 引导的从句强调持续性，常用进行时：While I **was sleeping**, the phone rang.
2. when 引导的从句动作可长可短：When I **got** home, my mother was cooking.
3. 灾害名词前常用 the：the earthquake, the fire；也可与 a/an 连用，视语境而定。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：______ I was doing my homework, the light went out.（A. When  B. While  C. As soon as）
2. 连词成句：was / cooking / mother / when / my / the / rang / telephone / she (.)
3. 用所给词适当形式填空：
   - When the fire alarm ______ (go) off, the students ______ (leave) the building quickly.
   - ______ (not run) during an earthquake. ______ (cover) your head and neck.
4. 汉译英：当地震发生时，你应该远离窗户，躲在坚固的家具下面。

**答案**：1. B  2. My mother was cooking when the telephone rang.  3. went; left; Don't run; Cover  4. When an earthquake happens, you should stay away from windows and hide under sturdy furniture.
