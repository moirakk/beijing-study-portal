# Unit 2 Improving yourself · 读写与表达（Developing ideas）

## 读写任务

本课围绕"自我提升的方法"展开读写训练。阅读文本介绍名人通过刻意练习实现突破的经历；写作任务为应用文（建议信）：**给苦恼于学习效率低的笔友 Jim 写一封建议信**，要求提出 2–3 条具体可行的建议并说明理由，语气友好诚恳。

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["开头：表达关心 + 点明来信目的"] --> B["主体：建议一 + 理由"]
    B --> C["主体：建议二 + 理由"]

```

## 写作模板与句式

**开头（表达关心与目的）**
- I'm sorry to hear that you are troubled by low study efficiency. As your friend, I'd like to offer some suggestions.
- Learning about your problem, I can't wait to share some tips that once worked for me.

**主体（提出建议与理由）**
- First and foremost, why not make a daily to-do list? Setting clear priorities helps you focus on what really matters.
- In addition, I strongly recommend taking short breaks between study sessions, which can refresh your mind and boost efficiency.
- What's more, it would be a good idea to reflect on your mistakes regularly, because reviewing errors prevents you from repeating them.

**结尾（鼓励与期待）**
- Rome wasn't built in a day, so be patient with yourself and stick to your plan.
- I'm sure these tips will work. Looking forward to hearing your good news!

**建议类高级句式**
- If I were you, I would start with small, achievable goals.
- Only by forming consistent habits can we make steady progress.

## 范文提纲

**题目**：A Letter of Advice on Improving Study Efficiency

1. **Para 1 开头**：表达同情与理解，点明写信目的（offer some practical suggestions）。
2. **Para 2 建议一**：制定学习计划、区分优先级（make a to-do list; set priorities）；理由：避免拖延、聚焦重点。
3. **Para 3 建议二**：劳逸结合与定期反思（take short breaks; reflect on mistakes）；理由：恢复精力、避免重复错误。
4. **Para 4 结尾**：鼓励坚持（perseverance pays off），期待回信。

## 背记要点

- 建议信三段式：关心目的 → 建议加理由 → 鼓励祝愿。
- 建议句式三件套：why not do / I strongly recommend doing / it would be a good idea to do。
- 每条建议必须配理由：..., which can ... / because ...。
- 高考视角：北京卷应用文常考建议信，注意语气友好、条理清晰。
- 谚语点睛：Rome wasn't built in a day. / Practice makes perfect.

## 自测题

1. 用 recommend doing 写一句关于时间管理的建议。
2. 写出建议信开头的一个常用句式。
3. 用 Only by ... can we ... 改写：We can make progress by forming good habits.
4. 列出建议信结尾的两个常用表达。

**答案**：1. 如 I strongly recommend making a daily schedule.  2. 如 I'm sorry to hear that ... I'd like to offer some suggestions.  3. Only by forming good habits can we make progress.  4. Looking forward to your reply. / I'm sure these tips will work.
