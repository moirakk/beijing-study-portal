# Unit 3 Times change! · 读写与表达（Developing ideas）

## 读写任务

本课围绕"家乡的变化"展开读写训练。阅读文本描写一座城市数十年间的今昔对比；写作任务为说明性短文/应用文：**向外国笔友介绍你家乡（北京）的变化**，要求今昔对比清晰、例证具体、表达自豪与思考。

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["开头：总起（家乡巨变）"] --> B["主体一：过去的面貌"]
    B --> C["主体二：如今的变化（交通/环境/科技）"]
    C --> D["结尾：感受与展望"]
```

## 写作模板与句式

**开头（总起）**
- Great changes have taken place in my hometown Beijing over the past decades.
- If you visited Beijing twenty years ago and returned today, you would hardly recognise it.

**主体（今昔对比）**
- In the past, people had to queue for hours to buy train tickets; nowadays, a few taps on the phone will do.
- Gone are the days when narrow roads were crowded with bicycles; wide streets and subway lines now stretch in all directions.
- What impresses visitors most is the harmony between modern skyscrapers and well-preserved hutongs.

**主体（例证与数据）**
- Take transportation as an example: the subway network has grown into one of the largest in the world.
- Thanks to years of effort, blue skies have become the new normal.

**结尾（感受与展望）**
- Witnessing these changes, I feel truly proud of my hometown.
- I firmly believe that Beijing will embrace an even brighter future while preserving its cultural heritage.

## 范文提纲

**题目**：Changes in My Hometown Beijing

1. **Para 1 总起**：点明主题——北京几十年来发生巨变（great changes have taken place）。
2. **Para 2 过去**：交通不便、通讯落后、环境问题（queue for tickets; narrow roads）。
3. **Para 3 现在**：地铁网络、移动支付、蓝天保卫战、胡同保护（mobile payment; blue skies; preserved hutongs）。
4. **Para 4 结尾**：自豪之情与展望——现代化与文化传承并行（pride; embrace the future while preserving heritage）。

## 背记要点

- 对比结构三件套：In the past ... nowadays ... / Gone are the days when ... / used to do。
- 例证句式：Take ... as an example / Thanks to ...。
- 结尾升华：现代化与传统保护并重，体现思辨性。
- 高考视角：介绍家乡变化是北京卷书面表达经典题型，注意时态（过去时与现在完成时交替）。
- 亮点句：If you visited ... and returned today, you would hardly recognise it.（虚拟条件句开头）

## 自测题

1. 用 Gone are the days when ... 写一句关于通讯方式变化的句子。
2. 写出"发生巨变"的两种英文表达。
3. 用 Take ... as an example 写一个关于交通变化的例证句。
4. 列出文章结尾表达自豪与展望的两个句式。

**答案**：1. 如 Gone are the days when we wrote letters and waited weeks for a reply.  2. Great changes have taken place. / The city has changed dramatically.  3. 如 Take transportation as an example: the subway now reaches almost every corner of the city.  4. Witnessing these changes, I feel proud of ... / I firmly believe that ... will embrace a brighter future.
