# Unit 4 Breaking boundaries · 读写与表达（Developing ideas）

## 读写任务

本课围绕"跨越边界的合作与交流"展开读写训练。阅读文本介绍国际志愿组织的援助行动；写作任务为应用文（倡议书/邀请信）：**以学生会名义写一份参加"国际文化交流周"志愿服务的倡议书**，要求说明活动意义、内容安排与报名方式，号召力强。

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["开头：点明活动 + 目的意义"] --> B["主体：活动内容与安排"]
    B --> C["主体：志愿者职责与收获"]
    C --> D["结尾：号召报名 + 联系方式"]
```

## 写作模板与句式

**开头（点明活动与意义）**
- To promote mutual understanding between cultures, our school will hold an International Culture Exchange Week from June 5 to 11.
- It is a golden opportunity to break down cultural barriers and make friends from around the world.

**主体（内容与职责）**
- The event includes cultural exhibitions, food festivals and language corners, where volunteers are badly needed.
- As a volunteer, you will guide foreign guests, introduce Chinese traditions and help with translation.
- Not only will you improve your spoken English, but you will also gain valuable cross-cultural experience.

**结尾（号召与方式）**
- Those interested please sign up at the Student Union office before May 30.
- Come and join us! Together we can build a bridge of friendship across boundaries.

**倡议类高级句式**
- Only by reaching out to different cultures can we truly broaden our horizons.
- Convinced that every effort counts, we sincerely invite you to take part.

## 范文提纲

**题目**：A Proposal for Volunteering in the International Culture Exchange Week

1. **Para 1 开头**：介绍活动名称、时间与宗旨（promote mutual understanding）。
2. **Para 2 内容**：展览、美食节、语言角；志愿者职责——导览、介绍传统、协助翻译（guide, introduce, translate）。
3. **Para 3 收获**：提升英语、开阔视野、结交朋友（Not only ... but also ... 倒装）。
4. **Para 4 结尾**：报名时间地点，发出号召（sign up before May 30; build a bridge of friendship）。

## 背记要点

- 倡议书四要素：活动信息 → 内容职责 → 参与收获 → 报名号召。
- 号召句式：Come and join us! / Don't hesitate to ... / Together we can ...。
- 倒装亮点：Not only will you ..., but you will also ...。
- 高考视角：北京卷应用文常考邀请信与倡议书，注意信息完整（时间、地点、方式）。
- 主题升华词：mutual understanding, a bridge of friendship, break down barriers。

## 自测题

1. 用 Not only ... but also ...（倒装）写一句关于志愿服务收获的句子。
2. 写出倡议书结尾的两个号召句式。
3. 用 Only by ... can we ... 表达"只有跨文化交流才能开阔视野"。
4. 列出倡议书必须包含的四类信息。

**答案**：1. 如 Not only will you improve your English, but you will also make new friends.  2. Come and join us! / Don't hesitate to sign up.  3. Only by communicating across cultures can we broaden our horizons.  4. 活动信息（时间地点）、内容职责、参与收获、报名方式。
