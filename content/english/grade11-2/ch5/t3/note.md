# Unit 5 A delicate world · 读写与表达（Developing ideas）

## 读写任务

本课围绕"保护脆弱的自然"展开读写训练。阅读文本介绍湿地生态修复的成功案例；写作任务为议论文/倡议文：**就"保护生物多样性，人人有责"写一篇短文**，要求观点明确、论证有层次（现状—原因—措施），结尾发出呼吁。

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["开头：现状引入（问题严峻）"] --> B["主体一：原因分析"]
    B --> C["主体二：解决措施（个人 + 社会）"]
    C --> D["结尾：呼吁行动"]
```

## 写作模板与句式

**开头（现状引入）**
- With species disappearing at an alarming rate, protecting biodiversity has become an urgent task.
- It is reported that thousands of species are on the edge of extinction due to human activities.

**主体（原因分析）**
- The causes are not hard to identify: pollution, deforestation and climate change all disturb the delicate balance of nature.
- What makes things worse is that many people remain unaware of the consequences.

**主体（措施建议）**
- For one thing, governments should establish more nature reserves and enforce stricter laws.
- For another, as individuals, we can start with small steps, such as saying no to plastic bags and saving energy.
- Were everyone to make a small change, the world would become a greener place.

**结尾（呼吁行动）**
- It is high time that we lived in harmony with nature.
- Only when we treat nature as a friend can we secure a sustainable future.

## 范文提纲

**题目**：Protecting Biodiversity: Everyone's Responsibility

1. **Para 1 现状**：物种加速消失，形势紧迫（at an alarming rate; on the edge of extinction）。
2. **Para 2 原因**：污染、砍伐、气候变化扰乱生态平衡；公众意识不足（disturb the balance; unaware）。
3. **Para 3 措施**：政府层面——建保护区、严立法；个人层面——绿色生活、拒绝塑料（nature reserves; small steps）。
4. **Para 4 呼吁**：与自然和谐共生，虚拟语气升华（It is high time that ...; Were everyone to ...）。

## 背记要点

- 议论文三段论：现状 → 原因 → 措施，结尾呼吁。
- 分层论证：For one thing ... For another ... / from the government's and individuals' perspectives。
- 虚拟语气亮点：It is high time that we lived in harmony with nature.
- 高考视角：环保议论文需数据感开头（It is reported that ...）与呼吁式结尾。
- 主题词库：biodiversity, ecosystem, sustainable, conservation, awareness。

## 自测题

1. 用 With + 名词 + doing 写一个环保话题的开头句。
2. 写出表达"措施分层"的两组衔接词。
3. 用虚拟语气写一句关于"人人行动"的升华句。
4. 列出议论文结尾呼吁的两个句式。

**答案**：1. 如 With forests shrinking year by year, wildlife is losing its home.  2. For one thing ... For another ... / On the one hand ... On the other hand ...  3. 如 Were everyone to plant one tree, the earth would be much greener.  4. It is high time that ... / Only when ... can we ...
