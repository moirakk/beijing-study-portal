# Unit 2 I became so bored with their orders that I wished they would leave me alone

标签：#Module4 #so...that #成长与理解 #阅读课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 4 的阅读课，课文是一个中学生的自述：父母外出前给"我"留下一大堆指令——做作业、喂猫、倒垃圾……"我"起初对这些命令感到厌烦（I became **so** bored with their orders **that** I wished they would leave me alone），巴不得他们赶快离开。可当真正独自在家后，"我"要自己做饭、洗衣、照顾一切，才体会到父母平日的辛劳，最终理解并感激父母。

文章采用**先抑后扬**的写法：从"厌烦唠叨"到"理解父母"，情感转变是理解主旨的关键，也是中考记叙文阅读的常见命题点。

关键句：
- I became **so bored with** their orders **that** I wished they would leave me alone. 我对他们的指令烦透了，希望他们别管我。
- **As soon as** they left, I turned on the television. 他们一走，我就打开了电视。
- Now I understand **how hard** my parents work for me. 现在我明白了父母为我付出多少辛劳。

## 二、重点词汇

- **order** n. 指令；命令  **bored** adj. 厌烦的
- **dirty** adj. 脏的  **wash** v. 洗
- **iron** v. 熨烫  **burn** v. 烧焦（burnt/burned）
- **be bored with** 对……厌烦  **leave sb. alone** 不打扰某人
- **turn on/off** 打开/关闭  **do some washing** 洗衣服
- **prepare meals** 做饭  **realise** v. 意识到
- **can't wait to do** 迫不及待做某事

## 三、语法要点

**so...that... 引导结果状语从句**

1. 结构：so + **形容词/副词** + that 从句，"如此……以至于……"：
   - He ran **so fast that** I couldn't catch up with him.
   - I was **so tired that** I fell asleep at once.
2. so + adj. + a/an + 单数名词 + that：It was **so interesting a book that** I read it twice.
3. 与 such...that 的转换：such + (a/an) + adj. + 名词 + that：
   - It was **such an interesting book that**... = It was so interesting a book that...
4. so...that 否定式可与 too...to 转换：
   - The box is **so** heavy **that** I **can't** carry it. = The box is **too** heavy **to** carry.

## 四、易错点

1. so 后接形容词/副词；such 后接名词（词组）：❌ so a nice day → ✅ such a nice day。
2. that 从句中主语与 too...to 转换时注意加 for sb.：so...that I can't... = too...for me to...
3. bored（人感到无聊）与 boring（事物令人无聊）不可混用。
4. leave me alone（别打扰我）≠ leave me lonely。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：The problem is ______ difficult ______ few students can work it out.（A. too; to  B. so; that  C. such; that  D. very; that）
2. 单项选择：It was ______ hot day that we all went swimming.（A. so  B. so a  C. such  D. such a）
3. 同义句转换：He is so young that he can't go to school. = He is ______ young ______ go to school.
4. 用所给词适当形式填空：I was ______ (bore) with doing the same work every day.
5. 汉译英：直到独自在家，我才意识到父母的工作有多辛苦。

**答案**：1. B  2. D  3. too; to  4. bored  5. I didn't realise how hard my parents worked until I stayed home alone.
