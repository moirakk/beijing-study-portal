# Unit 1 I can look after myself, although it won't be easy for me

标签：#Module4 #独自在家 #让步状语从句 #听说课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 4 Home alone 的听说课。对话情景：大明的父母要外出几天，大明将**独自在家**。父母临行前反复叮嘱：按时吃饭、不要整天玩电脑、有事给邻居打电话等；大明则向父母保证自己能照顾好自己（I can **look after myself**, although it won't be easy for me.）。

对话重点是 **although / though 引导的让步状语从句**，以及父母叮嘱时使用的祈使句与情态动词句式。

关键句：
- I can look after myself, **although** it won't be easy for me. 虽然不容易，但我能照顾好自己。
- **Though** I'm hungry, I won't eat junk food. 虽然我饿，但我不会吃垃圾食品。
- **Remember to** turn off the lights. 记得关灯。

## 二、重点词汇

- **alone** adv. 独自  **feed** v. 喂养（fed, fed）
- **pet** n. 宠物  **rubbish** n. 垃圾
- **empty** v. 倒空 adj. 空的  **fridge** n. 冰箱
- **look after** 照顾 = take care of
- **be/feel lonely** 感到孤独  **so far** 到目前为止
- **remember to do** 记得去做  **remember doing** 记得做过
- **make sure** 确保  **hand in** 上交

## 三、语法要点

**although / though 引导让步状语从句**

1. 意为"虽然；尽管"，引导让步状语从句，可位于句首或句中：
   - **Although** it was late, he went on working.
   - He went to school, **though** he was ill.
2. **不能与 but 连用**，但可与 still/yet 连用：
   - Although he is young, he **still** knows a lot.
3. though 可作副词放句末，意为"不过"：It's hard work. I enjoy it, **though**.

**alone 与 lonely**
- alone：客观"独自一人"（副词/表语形容词）：live alone。
- lonely：主观"孤独的"（可作定语）：feel lonely, a lonely island。

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ **Although**..., **but**... → 二者只能保留一个。
2. ❌ I feel **alone**. 表"孤独"应说 I feel **lonely**.
3. remember **to close** the door（未做）≠ remember **closing** the door（已做）。
4. although 从句同样遵循"主将从现"不适用——它不是时间/条件从句，从句时态按实际情况。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：______ it rained heavily, ______ they still went to work on time.（A. Although; but  B. Although; /  C. But; although  D. /; but）
2. 单项选择：Grandma lives ______, but she never feels ______.（A. alone; alone  B. lonely; lonely  C. alone; lonely  D. lonely; alone）
3. 完成句子：记得在离开前喂狗。______ ______ feed the dog before you leave.
4. 用所给词适当形式填空：He ______ (feed) the cat already. The cat is full now.
5. 汉译英：虽然独自在家不容易，但我能照顾好自己。

**答案**：1. B  2. C  3. Remember to  4. has fed  5. Although it's not easy to stay home alone, I can look after myself.
