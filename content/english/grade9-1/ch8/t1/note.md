# Unit 1 We're still influenced by Confucius's ideas

标签：#Module7 #伟大书籍 #被动语态 #听说课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 7 Great books 的听说课。学校杂志要评选"伟大的书"，Tony、玲玲和大明展开讨论：什么样的书才算伟大？玲玲介绍了**孔子（Confucius）**——他是伟大的思想家、教育家，《论语》记录了他的思想，**我们至今仍受孔子思想的影响**（We're still **influenced by** Confucius's ideas）；Tony 则提到了莎士比亚和马克·吐温的作品。

对话首次系统呈现**一般现在时的被动语态**（am/is/are + 过去分词），用于客观介绍书籍与思想的影响。

关键句：
- We're still **influenced by** Confucius's ideas today. 今天我们仍受孔子思想影响。
- His ideas **are known** in many countries. 他的思想闻名于许多国家。
- The books **are still read** by many people today. 这些书今天仍被许多人阅读。

## 二、重点词汇

- **influence** v./n. 影响  **thinker** n. 思想家
- **wise** adj. 智慧的（wisdom n.）  **education** n. 教育
- **discussion** n. 讨论  **idea** n. 思想；主意
- **be known as/for/to** 作为/因/为……所知
- **make a decision** 做决定  **all one's life** 终生
- **agree on** 就……达成一致  **come up with** 想出
- **thousands of** 数以千计的

## 三、语法要点

**一般现在时的被动语态**

1. 构成：**am / is / are + 过去分词**：
   - English **is spoken** in many countries.
   - These books **are read** all over the world.
2. 动作执行者用 **by** 引出（可省略）：
   - The magazine **is written by** students.
3. 主动变被动三步：宾语提前作主语 → 动词变 be + done → 原主语放 by 后。
   - People still read his books. → His books **are** still **read** (by people).

## 四、易错点

1. 被动语态 be 动词随**主语**和**时态**变化：The story **is** told... / The stories **are** told...
2. ❌ The book is **wrote** by him. → ✅ is **written**（不规则过去分词）。
3. be known **as** 身份（作为）；be known **for** 原因（因为）；be known **to** 对象（为……所知）。
4. 不及物动词无被动：❌ The accident was happened. → ✅ The accident happened.

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Chinese ______ by more and more people around the world today.（A. speaks  B. is spoken  C. is speaking  D. spoke）
2. 单项选择：Confucius is known ______ a great thinker and his ideas are known ______ many foreigners.（A. for; as  B. as; to  C. to; for  D. as; for）
3. 句型转换：People still read Lu Xun's books today.（改被动）Lu Xun's books ______ still ______ today.
4. 用所给词适当形式填空：The school magazine ______ (write) by the students themselves.
5. 汉译英：孔子的思想影响了中国两千多年。

**答案**：1. B  2. B  3. are; read  4. is written  5. Confucius's ideas have influenced China for more than two thousand years.
