# Unit 3 Language in use

标签：#Module3 #语法整合 #并列句 #人物描写

## 一、模块语法归纳

本单元整合 Module 3 Heroes 的语言点：**并列连词 and / but / or / so 构成并列句**，**few / a few / little / a little** 的用法，以及描写英雄人物生平的写作句式。

## 二、并列句系统梳理

**1. 四大并列连词**：
- and 顺承/并列：He finished school **and** became a doctor.
- but 转折：The work was dangerous, **but** he never stopped.
- or 选择/否则：Is he a doctor **or** a soldier? / Study hard, **or** you will fail.
- so 结果：She was well trained, **so** she played well.

**2. "祈使句 + and/or + 将来时"句型**：
- Work hard, **and** you'll succeed.（= If you work hard, you'll succeed.）
- Hurry up, **or** we'll miss the train.（= If you don't hurry, ...）

**3. 连词连用禁忌**：because ≠ so 同现；although ≠ but 同现。

## 三、few / little 复习表

- few + 可数复数（否定）｜ a few + 可数复数（肯定）
- little + 不可数（否定）｜ a little + 不可数（肯定）
- quite a few = many 相当多

## 四、人物传记写作句式积累

- ...was born in + 年份/地点
- As a child, he/she was interested in...
- He/She trained hard and never gave up.
- In one's twenties/thirties, ...
- He/She is remembered as a hero because...
- We are proud of... / ...set a good example to us.

## 五、易错点

1. "so + 形容词"与连词 so 区分：so brave（如此勇敢）≠ ..., so...（所以）。
2. ❌ a little students → ✅ a few students（student 可数）。
3. or 在否定句中相当于 and：He can't read **or** write.
4. 并列句前后时态、结构应保持一致（平行结构）。

## 六、练习题

1. 单项选择：Put on more clothes, ______ you will catch a cold.（A. and  B. but  C. or  D. so）
2. 单项选择：—Would you like some milk? —Yes, just ______, please.（A. few  B. a few  C. little  D. a little）
3. 单项选择：______ he was very tired, ______ he went on working.（A. Although; but  B. Although; /  C. /; because  D. Because; so）
4. 合并句子：Take this medicine. You will feel better soon.（用 and 连接）
5. 汉译英：他一生治疗了许多伤员，我们都记得这位英雄。

**答案**：1. C  2. D  3. B  4. Take this medicine, and you will feel better soon.  5. He treated many wounded soldiers in his life, and we all remember this hero.
