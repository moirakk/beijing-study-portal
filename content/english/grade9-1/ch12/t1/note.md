# Unit 1 He's the boy who won the photo competition last year

标签：#Module11 #摄影比赛 #定语从句who #听说课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 11 Photos 的听说课。学校举办摄影比赛，大家谈论参赛照片与选手：**他就是去年赢得摄影比赛的那个男孩**（He's the boy **who won the photo competition** last year）。对话围绕"哪张照片最好、谁拍的、拍的是什么"展开，比较各张照片的优缺点。

本单元语法在 that 定语从句的基础上引入**关系代词 who**（指人），并复习形容词比较级和最高级在评价照片时的运用。

关键句：
- He's the boy **who won the photo competition** last year. 他是去年摄影比赛的获胜者。
- The photo **which/that Lingling took** is the most beautiful. 玲玲拍的那张照片最漂亮。
- **Whose photo** do you like best? 你最喜欢谁的照片？

## 二、重点词汇

- **competition** n. 比赛（compete v.）  **prize** n. 奖品
- **photographer** n. 摄影师  **judge** n. 评委 v. 评判
- **exhibition** n. 展览  **scenery** n. 风景
- **win a prize** 获奖  **take part in** 参加
- **at the same time** 同时  **agree to do** 同意做
- **be pleased with** 对……满意  **hear about** 听说

## 三、语法要点

**who 引导的定语从句**

1. 先行词是**人**、关系代词作**主语**时，用 who（= that）：
   - The girl **who is smiling** is my sister.
   - He's the boy **who won** the competition.
2. 先行词是**物**时用 which（= that）：
   - The photo **which won** the first prize was taken by Daming.
3. 关系代词选择总结：
   - 指人：who / that；指物：which / that。
4. 从句谓语的数与先行词一致：the boys who **are**... / the boy who **is**...

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ The boy **which** won... → ✅ who/that（先行词是人）。
2. ❌ The photo **who** was taken... → ✅ which/that（先行词是物）。
3. who 作主语时从句不再加主语：❌ the boy who **he** won。
4. 最高级评价句：the most beautiful photo **of all**；比较级 + than any other + 单数名词。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Do you know the girl ______ is standing under the tree?（A. which  B. who  C. whom  D. what）
2. 单项选择：The photos ______ were taken by Tony are on show in the school hall.（A. who  B. whom  C. which  D. what）
3. 完成句子：获得一等奖的男孩是我的同班同学。The boy ______ ______ the first prize is my classmate.
4. 合并句子：The woman is a famous photographer. She is talking with our teacher.（用 who 合并）
5. 汉译英：我最喜欢的照片是大明在长城上拍的那张。

**答案**：1. B  2. C  3. who won  4. The woman who is talking with our teacher is a famous photographer.  5. The photo (that) I like best is the one (that) Daming took on the Great Wall.
