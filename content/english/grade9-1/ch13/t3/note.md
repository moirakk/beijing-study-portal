# Unit 3 Language in use

标签：#Module12 #语法整合 #unless #环保作文

## 一、模块语法归纳

本单元整合 Module 12 Save our world 的语言点：**unless 引导的条件状语从句**、被动语态在环保语境中的运用，并系统整理"环境保护"话题的中考写作素材。

## 二、条件状语从句大总结

1. **if**（如果）："主将从现"：If we save energy, we'll help the earth.
2. **unless**（除非）= if...not：
   - **Unless** we act now, it **will be** too late.
   - = **If** we **don't** act now, it will be too late.
3. 主句还可以是祈使句/情态动词句：
   - **Unless** you're tired, **let's** walk to school.

## 三、环保话题被动句集锦

- Rubbish **should be put** into different bins. 垃圾应分类投放。
- Plastic bags **shouldn't be used** too much. 塑料袋不应过度使用。
- Old clothes **can be given away** to people in need. 旧衣可捐给有需要的人。
- More trees **will be planted** next year. 明年将种更多的树。

## 四、中考环保作文模板句

**开头**：It's everyone's duty to protect the environment. / Our world is facing many problems.
**主体（建议）**：
- First, we should save water and electricity in our daily life.
- Second, don't throw away things unless they are really useless.
- Third, waste paper and bottles can be collected and recycled.
**结尾**：If everyone makes a contribution, the world will become better and better. / Actions speak louder than words.

## 五、易错点

1. unless 与 if not 转换时注意去掉 not，避免双重否定。
2. 建议句式搭配：should + 动词原形；It's + adj. + to do；Why not do...?
3. 被动语态中情态动词后 be 不可漏写。
4. make a contribution to + 名词/动名词（to 是介词）。

## 六、练习题

1. 单项选择：______ you take action to protect the environment, the pollution will get worse.（A. If  B. Unless  C. When  D. Until）
2. 单项选择：Old newspapers ______ to make new paper.（A. can use  B. can be used  C. can be use  D. must use）
3. 同义句转换：Unless you hurry up, you'll miss the train. = If you ______ ______ up, you'll miss the train.
4. 完成句子：保护地球是我们每个人的责任。It's ______ ______ to protect the earth.
5. 书面表达（提纲）：以 "How to Protect Our Environment" 为题列出三条建议，要求使用 unless、被动语态和 if 条件句各一次。

**答案**：1. B  2. B  3. don't hurry  4. everyone's duty  5. 略（参照模板句）
