# Unit 2 We have celebrated the festival since the first pioneers arrived in America

标签：#Module2 #感恩节 #since和for #阅读课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 2 的阅读课，课文介绍美国的**感恩节（Thanksgiving Day）**：它在每年**十一月的第四个星期四**，是感谢秋天丰收的节日。文章从节日的由来（1621 年第一批移民 pioneers 与印第安人共庆丰收）讲到今天人们的庆祝方式——家人团聚、吃火鸡（turkey）大餐、看橄榄球赛、观看感恩节游行等。

文章采用"**历史由来 → 现代庆祝 → 节日意义**"的说明结构，是介绍节日类文章的典型范文，可作为写作模板积累。

关键句：
- We **have celebrated** the festival **since** the first pioneers arrived in America. 自从第一批拓荒者到达美洲，我们就一直庆祝这个节日。
- Thanksgiving is a special day **for giving thanks** for the autumn harvest. 感恩节是为秋收感恩的特殊日子。
- Families and friends **get together** and have a big dinner. 家人朋友团聚，共进丰盛晚餐。

## 二、重点词汇

- **celebrate** v. 庆祝  **harvest** n. 收获；丰收
- **pioneer** n. 拓荒者；先驱  **turkey** n. 火鸡
- **pumpkin** n. 南瓜  **parade** n. 游行
- **decorate** v. 装饰  **recently** adv. 最近
- **depend on** 依靠；取决于  **get together** 团聚
- **give thanks for...** 为……而感恩
- **lay the table** 摆放餐具  **similar to** 与……相似

## 三、语法要点

**现在完成时 + since / for**

1. **since + 过去时间点/过去时从句**：
   - We have lived here **since 2015**. / **since we came here**.
2. **for + 一段时间**：
   - They have known each other **for ten years**.
3. 谓语动词必须是**延续性动词**才能与 since/for 连用：
   - ❌ He has **bought** the book for a week. → ✅ He has **had** the book for a week.
   - 常见转换：buy → have，borrow → keep，come → be here，die → be dead。

## 四、易错点

1. since 从句用一般过去时，主句用现在完成时：Since he **left**, I **have felt** lonely.
2. ❌ for 2015 / since ten years → ✅ **since** 2015 / **for** ten years。
3. "在几月"用 in November；"在具体某天"用 on Thanksgiving Day。
4. depend on 的 on 不可丢：It **depends on** the weather.

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Mr. Smith has worked in this school ______ he came to the city.（A. for  B. since  C. when  D. after）
2. 单项选择：—How long have you ______ the bike? —For about two weeks.（A. bought  B. had  C. buy  D. have）
3. 用所给词适当形式填空：People ______ (celebrate) Thanksgiving since the 17th century.
4. 完成句子：感恩节取决于收获的季节。Thanksgiving ______ ______ the harvest season.
5. 汉译英：每年十一月的第四个星期四，美国家庭都会团聚在一起吃火鸡。

**答案**：1. B  2. B  3. have celebrated  4. depends on  5. On the fourth Thursday of November every year, American families get together and eat turkey.
