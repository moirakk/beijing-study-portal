# Unit 3 Language in use

标签：#Module2 #语法整合 #现在完成时 #时间状语从句

## 一、模块语法归纳

本单元整合 Module 2 Public holidays 的核心语法：**现在完成时与 since/for 连用**、**as soon as 引导的时间状语从句**，并归纳谈论节假日的功能句型。

## 二、现在完成时系统梳理

**1. 构成**：have/has + 过去分词

**2. 两大用法**：
- 影响性用法：过去动作对现在造成影响，常与 already, yet, just, ever, never 连用。
  - I **have already finished** my plans for the holiday.
- 延续性用法：动作从过去持续到现在，与 since/for 连用。
  - She **has been** a teacher **for** twenty years.

**3. since 与 for 辨析**：
| 引导词 | 后接内容 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| since | 时间点 / 过去时从句 | since 2010; since I was born |
| for | 一段时间 | for three days |

**4. 短暂性动词不能直接与 for/since 连用**，需转为延续性：join → be in / be a member of，leave → be away (from)。

## 三、时间状语从句"主将从现"

- I'll send you a photo **as soon as I arrive**.
- **When** the holiday **comes**, we **will visit** our grandparents.
- 适用连词：when, as soon as, before, after, until, if（条件句同理）。

## 四、重点短语与句型

- **have...off** 放假  **get together** 团聚
- **depend on** 取决于  **give thanks for** 为……感恩
- **all kinds of** 各种各样的  **at the end of** 在……末尾
- **It's + 时间段 + since...** 自从……已有多久：It's five years since we met.

## 五、易错点

1. ❌ He has joined the club **for** two years. → ✅ He has **been in** the club for two years.
2. since 从句时态：since + **一般过去时**（❌ since he has come）。
3. 主将从现只管**从句**，主句该用将来时就用将来时。
4. How long 提问现在完成时的延续时间；When 提问过去时间点。

## 六、练习题

1. 单项选择：—How long have you ______ here? —Since three years ago.（A. come  B. arrived  C. lived  D. left）
2. 单项选择：Tom will call me as soon as he ______ Shanghai.（A. reaches  B. will reach  C. reached  D. is reaching）
3. 句型转换：He came to Beijing five years ago.（同义句）He ______ ______ in Beijing ______ five years.
4. 用所给词适当形式填空：My parents ______ (be) married for twenty years.
5. 汉译英：自从来到这所学校，我交了很多朋友。

**答案**：1. C  2. A  3. has been; for  4. have been  5. I have made many friends since I came to this school.
