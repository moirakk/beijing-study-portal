# Unit 1 My family always go somewhere interesting as soon as the holiday begins

标签：#Module2 #公共假日 #时间状语从句 #听说课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 2 Public holidays 的听说课。对话围绕美国的**劳动节（Labor Day）**展开：Betty 向大明介绍美国人如何度过劳动节假期——野餐、旅行、看比赛等，并谈到自己家的假期计划。

对话核心语言功能是**谈论节假日安排**，重点句型是由 **as soon as / when / while** 引导的时间状语从句，以及"主将从现"规则的自然运用。

关键句：
- My family always go somewhere interesting **as soon as** the holiday begins. 假期一开始，我们全家总是去有趣的地方。
- **When** we have a day off, we often have a picnic. 放假时我们常去野餐。
- I'll call you **as soon as** I get there. 我一到那儿就给你打电话。

## 二、重点词汇

- **public** adj. 公共的  **holiday** n. 假日
- **relative** n. 亲戚  **picnic** n. 野餐
- **fair** n. 集市；游乐会  **season** n. 季节
- **as soon as** 一……就……  **have a picnic** 举行野餐
- **have (a day) off** 放（一天）假
- **take a vacation** 度假（美式）  **all kinds of** 各种各样的
- **at the end of** 在……结束时  **make plans** 制订计划
- **somewhere interesting** 有趣的地方（形容词后置）

## 三、语法要点

**as soon as 引导的时间状语从句**

1. 意为"一……就……"，强调两个动作紧接着发生：
   - **As soon as** he arrived, he called me.
2. **主将从现**：主句是一般将来时，从句用一般现在时表将来：
   - I'll tell her the news **as soon as she comes** back.（❌ will come）
3. 类似规则也适用于 when, before, after, until, if 引导的从句。

**不定代词 + 形容词后置**
- somewhere/something/anything 等 + 形容词：somewhere **interesting**, something **special**。

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ I'll write to you as soon as I **will get** to Beijing. → ✅ ...as soon as I **get** to Beijing.
2. ❌ **interesting somewhere** → ✅ somewhere **interesting**（形容词后置）。
3. family 表"家人"时谓语用复数：My family **are** all at home.；表"家庭"整体时用单数。
4. vacation（美）与 holiday（英）同义，注意语境搭配 take a vacation / on holiday。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：I'll give the book to him as soon as he ______ back.（A. comes  B. will come  C. came  D. is coming）
2. 单项选择：We are going ______ for the summer holiday.（A. warm somewhere  B. warm anywhere  C. somewhere warm  D. anywhere warmly）
3. 完成句子：他一到家就开始做作业。He began to do his homework ______ ______ ______ he got home.
4. 用所给词适当形式填空：When we have a day ______ (off/of), we usually visit our relatives.
5. 汉译英：假期一开始，我们就会制订旅行计划。

**答案**：1. A  2. C  3. as soon as  4. off  5. As soon as the holiday begins, we will make plans for our trip.
