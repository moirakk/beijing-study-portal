# Unit 1 Will computers be used more than books in the future?

标签：#Module9 #伟大发明 #将来时被动 #听说课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 9 Great inventions 的听说课。对话围绕"伟大的发明"展开：老师请同学们讨论书籍、电脑、电视等发明对学习和生活的影响，并展望未来——**将来电脑会比书籍使用得更多吗？**（**Will** computers **be used** more than books in the future?）同学们各抒己见：有人认为纸质书不会消失，有人认为网络学习将成为主流。

对话的语法重点是**一般将来时的被动语态**（will be + 过去分词），语言功能是谈论发明与预测未来。

关键句：
- **Will** computers **be used** more than books in the future? 未来电脑会比书用得更多吗？
- Books **will still be read** by many people. 书仍会被许多人阅读。
- The Internet **was invented** in the 20th century. 互联网发明于20世纪。

## 二、重点词汇

- **invention** n. 发明（invent v., inventor n.）
- **create** v. 创造  **appear** v. 出现
- **electricity** n. 电  **telephone** n. 电话
- **satellite** n. 卫星  **program** n. 程序；节目
- **be connected to** 与……相连  **search for** 搜索
- **communicate with** 与……交流  **in the future** 在将来
- **more and more** 越来越多  **thanks to** 多亏了

## 三、语法要点

**一般将来时的被动语态**

1. 构成：**will be + 过去分词**：
   - More satellites **will be sent** into space.
   - The work **will be finished** tomorrow.
2. 否定式：won't be + 过去分词：Books **won't be replaced** completely.
3. 疑问式：Will + 主语 + be + 过去分词...?
   - **Will** robots **be used** in every home?
4. be going to 被动：is/are going to be done：The bridge **is going to be built** next year.

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ will be **use** → ✅ will be **used**（漏过去分词标志）。
2. ❌ will **been** used → ✅ will **be** used（will 后接原形 be）。
3. 主动表将来的预测：I think... / I believe... 后接从句否定前移：I **don't think** books will disappear.
4. more than 比较时两边对象要一致：computers ... more than **books**。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：In the future, more robots ______ in factories.（A. will use  B. will be used  C. are used  D. were used）
2. 单项选择：—Will paper books disappear one day? —I ______ so. People still enjoy reading them.（A. don't think  B. think not  C. don't believe it  D. not think）
3. 句型转换：People will hold the meeting next Friday.（改被动）The meeting ______ ______ ______ next Friday.
4. 用所给词适当形式填空：The telephone ______ (invent) by Bell in 1876.
5. 汉译英：多亏了互联网，信息能被快速地搜索到。

**答案**：1. B  2. A  3. will be held  4. was invented  5. Thanks to the Internet, information can be searched for quickly.
