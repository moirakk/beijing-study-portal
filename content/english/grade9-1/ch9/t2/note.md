# Unit 2 He was invited to competitions around the world

标签：#Module8 #刘翔 #过去时被动 #阅读课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 8 的阅读课，课文介绍中国著名跨栏运动员**刘翔（Liu Xiang）**的体育生涯：他少年时先练跳高，后来教练发现他跨栏的天赋；2004 年雅典奥运会上，他以 12.91 秒平世界纪录的成绩夺得 110 米栏金牌，成为第一个在奥运短跑项目上夺冠的亚洲人；此后**他被邀请参加世界各地的比赛**（He **was invited to** competitions around the world）。文章也提到他因伤退赛的挫折与坚持。

文章综合运用一般过去时的主动与被动语态叙述运动员生平，是体育人物传记的典型文本。

关键句：
- He **was invited to** competitions around the world. 他被邀请参加世界各地的比赛。
- He **was trained** by a top coach. 他接受了顶级教练的训练。
- His record **was broken** several years later. 几年后他的纪录被打破。

## 二、重点词汇

- **record** n. 纪录 v. 记录  **race** n. 赛跑；比赛
- **gold** n. 金牌；黄金  **event** n. 比赛项目；事件
- **hurdle** n. 跨栏  **athlete** n. 运动员
- **retire** v. 退役；退休  **injury** n. 受伤
- **break the record** 打破纪录  **set a record** 创造纪录
- **win a gold medal** 获得金牌  **at the age of** 在……岁时
- **be famous for** 因……而出名  **because of** 因为（+名词）

## 三、语法要点

**一般过去时被动语态（巩固）**

1. was/were + 过去分词，常见于人物经历叙述：
   - He **was born** in Shanghai in 1983.
   - He **was chosen** to train as a hurdler.
2. 被动句中的介词搭配保留：be invited **to**..., be known **as**...
3. 主动与被动交替叙事：He **won** the gold medal, and he **was welcomed** home as a hero.

**because of 与 because**
- because + 从句；because of + 名词/代词/动名词：
  - He retired **because of** his injury. = He retired **because** he was injured.

## 四、易错点

1. ❌ He was **breaked** the record. → 主动：He **broke** the record；被动只能说 The record **was broken** by him.
2. win 赢得（比赛/奖牌）；beat 战胜（对手）：win the match / beat the team。
3. at the age of 14 = when he was 14 years old。
4. set a record（创纪录）与 break a record（破纪录）搭配不可混。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Liu Xiang ______ the world record ______ the 110m hurdles in 2006.（A. broke; in  B. was broken; in  C. broke; of  D. beat; in）
2. 单项选择：Our team ______ theirs in the final and we won the game.（A. won  B. beat  C. was beaten  D. was won）
3. 单项选择：He didn't go to school ______ his illness.（A. because  B. because of  C. so  D. since）
4. 句型转换：The coach invited him to join the national team.（改被动）He ______ ______ to join the national team by the coach.
5. 汉译英：他在 21 岁时获得了奥运金牌，被全国人民视为英雄。

**答案**：1. A  2. B  3. B  4. was invited  5. He won an Olympic gold medal at the age of 21 and was seen as a hero by the whole country.
