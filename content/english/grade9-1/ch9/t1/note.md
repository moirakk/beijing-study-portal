# Unit 1 Daming wasn't chosen for the team last time

标签：#Module8 #体育生活 #过去时被动 #听说课

## 一、课文精讲

本单元是 Module 8 Sports life 的听说课。对话围绕学校篮球队选拔展开：**大明上次没有被选进校队**（Daming **wasn't chosen** for the team last time），他有点沮丧，但朋友们鼓励他坚持训练，这次再去参加选拔。对话还谈到了著名篮球运动员的成长经历——他们也曾多次落选，靠坚持不懈获得成功。

对话的语法重点是**一般过去时的被动语态**（was/were + 过去分词），语言功能是**鼓励他人**。

关键句：
- Daming **wasn't chosen** for the team last time. 大明上次没被选进队。
- **Bad luck!** 真倒霉！
- **Don't give up!** Keep trying and you'll make it. 别放弃！继续努力你会成功的。
- He **was encouraged** by his coach to train harder. 教练鼓励他更刻苦地训练。

## 二、重点词汇

- **choose** v. 挑选（chose, chosen）  **club** n. 俱乐部
- **training** n. 训练  **member** n. 成员
- **stand** v. 忍受；站立  **cheer** v. 欢呼
- **be chosen for** 被选入  **give up** 放弃
- **make it** 成功；做到  **play against** 与……比赛
- **cheer sb. on** 为某人加油  **from now on** 从现在起
- **have a chance to do** 有机会做某事

## 三、语法要点

**一般过去时的被动语态**

1. 构成：**was / were + 过去分词**：
   - The match **was won** by our school team.
   - The players **were chosen** last week.
2. 否定式：was/were + not + 过去分词：
   - He **wasn't chosen** for the team.
3. 疑问式：Was/Were + 主语 + 过去分词...?
   - **Were** you **invited** to the game?

**鼓励用语**：Bad luck! / Don't give up! / Keep trying! / You'll make it next time! / Well done!

## 四、易错点

1. choose 的变化：choose - **chose** - **chosen**，注意拼写区分。
2. 被动语态时态由 be 体现：was chosen（过去）≠ is chosen（现在）。
3. ❌ He wasn't **choose**... → ✅ wasn't **chosen**（必须用过去分词）。
4. play against sb. 与某人对抗比赛；play with sb. 和某人一起玩。

## 五、练习题

1. 单项选择：Lin Tao ______ for the school team because he played basketball very well.（A. chose  B. was chosen  C. is chosen  D. chosen）
2. 单项选择：—I failed the singing competition again. —______! Keep trying and you'll succeed.（A. Congratulations  B. Well done  C. Don't give up  D. That's all right）
3. 句型转换：They didn't invite Daming to the party.（改被动）Daming ______ ______ to the party.
4. 用所给词适当形式填空：The players ______ (train) by a famous coach last year.
5. 汉译英：虽然他上次没被选上，但他从未放弃训练。

**答案**：1. B  2. C  3. wasn't invited  4. were trained  5. Although he wasn't chosen last time, he never gave up training.
