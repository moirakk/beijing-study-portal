# Unit 6·重点梳理

标签：#Unit6 #语法梳理 #现在进行时 #形容词

## 一、本单元语法总览

Unit 6 的语法核心：**现在进行时（be + doing）**、**形容词描述人（巩固）**、**并列连词 and/but/or**。现在进行时是七上收官语法，也是期末大题重点。

## 二、现在进行时

1. **含义**：表示"此刻正在进行"的动作，常与 now, look, listen, at the moment 连用。
   - Look! Tony **is playing** football on the playground.
   - They **are talking** about their fantastic friends now.
2. **结构**：主语 + **am/is/are + 动词-ing**。
   - I **am doing** my homework. / She **is reading**. / We **are singing**.
3. **动词 -ing 变化规则**：
   - 直接加：do → doing, play → playing；
   - 去 e 加：write → writing, make → making, have → having；
   - 双写加：run → running, swim → swimming, sit → sitting, get → getting。
4. **否定与疑问**：
   - 否定：She **isn't listening** to music.
   - 一般疑问：**Are** you **watching** TV? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
   - 特殊疑问：What **are** you **doing**? — I'm drawing a picture.
5. **与一般现在时对比**（高频考点）：
   - 一般现在时表**经常性**动作：He **plays** football every day.
   - 现在进行时表**此刻**动作：He **is playing** football now.
   - 信号词：every day / usually / often → 一般现在时；now / look / listen → 现在进行时。
6. **常见错误对比**：
   - ❌ She reading a book. → ✅ She **is** reading a book.（漏 be）
   - ❌ He is play basketball. → ✅ He is **playing** basketball.（漏 -ing）
   - ❌ Look! They swim in the river. → ✅ Look! They **are swimming** ...

## 三、并列连词 and / but / or

- **and** 并列递进：She is kind **and** helpful.
- **but** 转折：He is shy, **but** he is friendly.
- **or** 选择；否定句中的"和"：I don't like maths **or** science.

## 四、需要记住的搭配

1. get on well with sb 与某人相处融洽
2. help each other 互相帮助
3. at the moment = now 此刻
4. talk with/to sb 与某人交谈；talk about sth 谈论某事
5. one... the other...（两者中）一个……另一个……

## 五、考点与易错点

- 现在进行时**三查**：查 be、查 -ing、查主语一致，缺一不可。
- 双写规则单词（running, swimming, sitting, getting, shopping）是词形填空高频。
- Look! / Listen! 开头的句子九成考现在进行时。
- 否定句中并列用 **or** 不用 and：I can't sing or dance.

## 六、小练习

1. Listen! Lingling ______ in the music room.（A. sings B. is singing C. singing）
2. — What are you doing? — I ______ a letter to my friend.（A. write B. am writing C. writes）
3. Look at the boys! They ______ in the river.（A. swim B. are swiming C. are swimming）
4. My best friend is quiet, ______ I am outgoing.（A. or B. and C. but）
5. He usually ______ to school by bike, but today he ______ to school.（A. goes; is walking B. go; walks C. is going; walks）
6. 汉译英：看！孩子们正在操场上跑步。

**答案**：1. B 2. B 3. C（双写 m）4. C 5. A（usually 配一般现在时，today 此刻配进行时）6. Look! The children are running on the playground.
