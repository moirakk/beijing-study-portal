# Unit 3·综合练习

标签：#Unit3 #单元测试 #综合练习

## 一、练习说明

覆盖 Unit 3 核心：家庭成员词汇、三单、指示代词、名词所有格。建议限时 25 分钟。

## 二、单项选择

1. — Who's the girl on the left? — She's my ______, my aunt's daughter.
   A. sister B. cousin C. mother
2. ______ is my mother, and ______ are my grandparents over there.
   A. This; those B. These; that C. This; that
3. My brother ______ to school by bike every day.
   A. go B. goes C. going
4. — ______ your father ______ dinner at home? — No, he doesn't.
   A. Do; cook B. Does; cooks C. Does; cook
5. This is ______ room. It's big and clean.
   A. Lucy and Lily B. Lucy's and Lily's C. Lucy and Lily's
6. My grandma often ______ stories to me in the evening.
   A. tells B. tell C. telling
7. — What does your mother do? — ______
   A. She likes reading. B. She is a nurse. C. She is fine.
8. My parents ______ me very much, and I love ______ too.
   A. love; they B. loves; them C. love; them

## 三、用所给词的适当形式填空

1. My father ______ (have) a new car.
2. Tony ______ (study) Chinese every evening.
3. My aunt ______ (not work) in a hospital. She's a teacher.
4. Look at these ______ (photo) of my family.
5. That is my ______ (grandparent) house.

## 四、按要求改写句子

1. My uncle works in Beijing.（改为一般疑问句）→ ______
2. She does her homework after dinner.（改为否定句）→ ______
3. My father is a doctor.（对画线部分提问，画线：a doctor）→ ______
4. of, is, a, this, family, photo, my（连词成句）→ ______

## 五、汉译英

1. 我家有六口人。
2. 我叔叔是一名司机，他工作很努力。
3. 你妈妈做什么工作？——她是一名护士。
4. 我奶奶总是照顾我们，我们都爱她。

## 六、参考答案

**二、单项选择**
1. B（姑姑的女儿是 cousin）
2. A（近处单数 this，远处复数 those）
3. B（三单 goes）
4. C（does 提问，动词还原 cook）
5. C（两人共有一间房，只在后一个名字后加 's）
6. A（三单 tells）
7. B（问职业答职业）
8. C（parents 复数配 love；宾语用 them）

**三、词形填空**
1. has 2. studies（变 y 为 i 加 es）3. doesn't work 4. photos 5. grandparents'

**四、改写句子**
1. Does your uncle work in Beijing?
2. She doesn't do her homework after dinner.
3. What does your father do?
4. This is a photo of my family.

**五、汉译英**
1. There are six people in my family.
2. My uncle is a driver. He works very hard.
3. What does your mother do? — She is a nurse.
4. My grandma always takes care of us. We all love her.

## 七、易错提醒

- 第 4 题：Does 出现后动词必须还原，B 选项 cooks 是典型陷阱。
- 词形填空第 2 题：study 三单变 **studies**；第 5 题复数所有格 s 后只加撇号。
- 汉译英第 1 题："有几口人"用 There be，不用 have。
