# Unit 5·综合练习

标签：#Unit5 #单元测试 #综合练习

## 一、练习说明

覆盖 Unit 5 核心：植物词汇、可数不可数、there be、some/any。建议限时 25 分钟。

## 二、单项选择

1. Rice ______ from a kind of plant.
   A. come B. comes C. coming
2. There ______ some bread and two eggs on the plate.
   A. is B. are C. have
3. Would you like ______ orange juice?
   A. some B. any C. many
4. We don't have ______ vegetables at home. Let's buy some.
   A. some B. any C. much
5. Plants can give ______ oxygen and make the air fresh.
   A. up B. out C. in
6. — ______ are the leaves in autumn? — They're yellow.
   A. What colour B. What C. How many
7. There are all ______ of plants in the garden.
   A. kind B. kinds C. a kind
8. How ______ mooncakes are there in the box? / How ______ milk is there in the glass?
   A. many; much B. much; many C. many; many

## 三、用所给词的适当形式填空

1. Look! There are many ______ (leaf) on the ground.
2. Plants are very ______ (use) to humans.
3. There ______ (be) a lot of rice in the bag.
4. My grandpa ______ (grow) vegetables in his garden.
5. Would you like two ______ (glass) of water?

## 四、按要求改写句子

1. There are some birds in the tree.（改为一般疑问句）→ ______
2. There is much water in the bottle.（改为否定句）→ ______
3. There are twenty trees in our school.（对画线部分提问，画线：twenty）→ ______
4. without, live, we, plants, can't（连词成句）→ ______

## 五、汉译英

1. 植物给我们食物、氧气和药物。
2. 公园里有各种各样的花。
3. 冰箱里没有牛奶了。
4. 我们应该多种树，保护环境。

## 六、参考答案

**二、单项选择**
1. B（rice 不可数作单数）
2. A（就近原则，bread 不可数）
3. A（表建议的疑问句用 some）
4. B（否定句用 any）
5. B（give out 释放）
6. A（问颜色 What colour）
7. B（all kinds of）
8. A（mooncakes 可数用 many；milk 不可数用 much）

**三、词形填空**
1. leaves 2. useful 3. is 4. grows 5. glasses

**四、改写句子**
1. Are there any birds in the tree?
2. There isn't much water in the bottle.
3. How many trees are there in your school?
4. We can't live without plants.

**五、汉译英**
1. Plants give us food, oxygen and medicine.
2. There are all kinds of flowers in the park.
3. There isn't any milk in the fridge.
4. We should plant more trees and protect the environment.

## 七、易错提醒

- 第 2 题：there be 就近原则 + bread 不可数，双重考点。
- 第 3 题：Would you like...? 表建议委婉请求，例外地用 some。
- 词形填空第 5 题：glass 复数是 glass**es**。
