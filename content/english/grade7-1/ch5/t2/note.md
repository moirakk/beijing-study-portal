# Unit 4·重点梳理

标签：#Unit4 #语法梳理 #时间介词 #一般现在时复习

## 一、本单元语法总览

Unit 4 的语法核心：**时间介词 in / on / at**、**一般现在时描述节日习俗（复习巩固三单）**、**when 引导的特殊疑问句**。

## 二、时间介词 in / on / at

| 介词 | 用法 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| in | 年、月、季节、世纪；泛指上午/下午/晚上 | in 2025, in May, in spring, in the morning |
| on | 具体某一天、某天的上下午 | on Monday, on July 1st, on Sunday morning |
| at | 时刻、节日期间（不含 Day） | at 7:00, at noon, at night, at Christmas |

**口诀**：at 时刻，on 具体某一天，in 大段时间（年月季）。

**易错对比**：
- ❌ in Monday morning → ✅ **on** Monday morning（某天的上午用 on）
- ❌ at the evening → ✅ **in** the evening（但 at night 固定）
- at Christmas（圣诞期间）vs on Christmas Day（圣诞当天）

## 三、when / what time 提问时间

- **When** is the Spring Festival? — It's in January or February.
- **What time** do you get up? — At six thirty.（what time 问具体钟点）
- when 可问钟点也可问日期；what time 只问钟点。

## 四、一般现在时表习俗（复习）

描述节日里"人们通常做什么"，用一般现在时：

- People **make** dumplings and **stay** up late at the Spring Festival.
- 主语三单时别忘加 s：Everyone **enjoys** the family dinner.（everyone 作单数）
- 频度副词复习：People **usually** give children lucky money.

## 五、需要记住的搭配

1. It's time to do sth = It's time for sth 该做某事了：It's time **to have** dinner. = It's time **for** dinner.
2. give sb sth = give sth to sb：give **me** a gift = give a gift **to** me
3. put up 挂起、张贴：put up lanterns / pictures
4. get ready for... 为……做准备：get ready **for** the festival
5. on one's birthday 在某人生日那天

## 六、考点与易错点

- in/on/at 选择题是期中期末**必考题**，重点盯"某天的上午"（on Sunday morning）。
- It's time to **do**（接动词原形）/ It's time for + **名词**，两个结构不能混搭。
- everyone / everybody 作主语按**单数**处理。
- 序数词前一般加 the：**the** first day of the new year。

## 七、小练习

1. We usually have a party ______ the evening of December 31st.（A. in B. on C. at）
2. — What time do you have breakfast? — ______ seven o'clock.（A. In B. On C. At）
3. It's time ______ home now.（A. to go B. going C. for go）
4. Everyone in my family ______ the Spring Festival.（A. love B. loves C. loving）
5. My birthday is ______ May, and my mother's birthday is ______ May 4th.（A. in; on B. on; in C. in; in）
6. 汉译英：中秋节在九月或十月，人们在那天晚上赏月。

**答案**：1. B（具体某天的晚上用 on）2. C 3. A 4. B 5. A 6. The Mid-Autumn Festival is in September or October. People watch the moon on that evening/night.
