# Starter·重点梳理

标签：#Starter #语法梳理 #be动词 #人称代词

## 一、本单元语言点总览

Starter 单元围绕"入学问候与自我介绍"，需要掌握三大语法基石：**be 动词（am/is/are）**、**人称代词主格**、**形容词性物主代词（my/your/his/her）**，以及数字 0–20。

## 二、be 动词：am / is / are

**口诀**：我用 am，你用 are，is 连着他她它；单数 is，复数 are。

| 主语 | be 动词 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I **am** a student. |
| you / we / they | are | You **are** in Class 2. |
| he / she / it / 单数名词 | is | She **is** my teacher. |

**缩写形式**：I'm = I am ｜ you're = you are ｜ he's = he is ｜ she's = she is ｜ it's = it is ｜ isn't = is not ｜ aren't = are not（注意：am not 没有缩写 *amn't*）

**三种句式转换**：

- 肯定：She is from England.
- 否定：She **is not (isn't)** from England.
- 一般疑问：**Is she** from England? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

❌ 错句对比：*I is a student.* → ✅ I **am** a student. ｜ *Are he your friend?* → ✅ **Is** he your friend?

## 三、人称代词与物主代词

| 人称 | 主格（作主语） | 形容词性物主代词（+名词） |
|---|---|---|
| 第一人称单/复 | I / we | my / our |
| 第二人称 | you | your |
| 第三人称 | he / she / it / they | his / her / its / their |

- **my、your、his、her 后必须接名词**：This is **my book**.（不能只说 This is my.）
- he 指男性，she 指女性，it 指物或动物。

❌ *He name is Tom.* → ✅ **His** name is Tom.

## 四、数字 0–20

zero, one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve, **thirteen**, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, **twenty**

- 13–19 以 **-teen** 结尾；注意特殊拼写 **thirteen**（不是 threeteen）、**fifteen**（不是 fiveteen）、**eighteen**（只有一个 t）。
- 问年龄：How old are you? — I'm twelve (years old).
- 问电话号码：What's your phone number? — It's 138....（0 读作 zero 或 oh）

## 五、冠词 a / an 初步

- a 用于**辅音音素**开头的词前：a book, a pen, a student；
- an 用于**元音音素**开头的词前：an apple, an egg, an English teacher。
- 看**发音**不看字母：a **u**niversity（/j/ 辅音音素）；an **h**our（h 不发音）。

## 六、考点与易错点

1. be 动词与主语一致是最高频考点（单选、用所给词适当形式填空）。
2. 一般疑问句要把 be 提前，句末用问号，答语 Yes/No 后的代词与 be 要匹配：Is this your pen? — Yes, **it is**.（不说 Yes, this is.）
3. this is 不能缩写为 ~~this's~~；that is 可缩写为 that's。
4. 汉译英常考："我十二岁" = I'm twelve. （❌ I have twelve.）

## 七、小练习

1. I ______ a student and my sister ______ a teacher.（am / is / are 填空）
2. — ______ you from Beijing? — Yes, I ______.
3. ______ name is Lucy and ______ is my good friend.（A. Her; she B. She; her C. Her; her）
4. 用 a / an 填空：______ orange, ______ ruler, ______ hour
5. 汉译英：他是我的英语老师，他来自英国。
6. 改为一般疑问句并作否定回答：They are in Class 5.

**答案**：1. am; is 2. Are; am 3. A 4. an; a; an 5. He is my English teacher. He is from England/the UK. 6. Are they in Class 5? — No, they aren't.
