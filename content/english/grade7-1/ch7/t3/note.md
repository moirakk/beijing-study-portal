# Unit 6·综合练习

标签：#Unit6 #单元测试 #综合练习 #期末复习

## 一、练习说明

覆盖 Unit 6 核心：外貌性格词汇、look like / be like、现在进行时、连词。本单元也是七上期末总复习入口，建议限时 25 分钟。

## 二、单项选择

1. — What ______ your new friend ______? — She's tall and thin.
   A. is; like B. does; look like C. does; like
2. Look! The girls ______ on the playground.
   A. dance B. are dancing C. dances
3. My friend Tony ______ glasses.
   A. wear B. wears C. wearing
4. — ______ you listening to music now? — Yes, I ______.
   A. Are; am B. Do; do C. Are; do
5. Betty is very ______. She always helps others.
   A. shy B. helpful C. quiet
6. We are good friends. We help ______ and learn from ______.
   A. each other; each other B. other; other C. each; each
7. I like English and history, but my brother doesn't like English ______ history.
   A. and B. but C. or
8. He is sitting there quietly, but he ______ a funny boy in fact.
   A. is B. are C. be

## 三、用所给词的适当形式填空

1. Look! My cousin ______ (swim) in the pool.
2. Listen! Someone ______ (sing) in the next room.
3. They ______ (make) cards for their friends now.
4. My best friend is ______ (friend) to everyone.
5. He usually ______ (run) in the morning, but now he ______ (sit) in the classroom.

## 四、按要求改写句子

1. They are playing chess now.（改为一般疑问句）→ ______
2. She is doing her homework.（对画线部分提问，画线：doing her homework）→ ______
3. He is watching TV at the moment.（改为否定句）→ ______
4. friend, is, always, my, there, best, me, for（连词成句）→ ______

## 五、汉译英

1. 你最好的朋友长什么样？——她个子高，戴眼镜。
2. 看！他们正在教室里画画。
3. 我们俩都喜欢音乐，但是我们最喜欢的歌手不同。
4. 他风趣又诚实，总能逗我笑。

## 六、参考答案

**二、单项选择**
1. B（答语为外貌，用 look like）
2. B（Look! 提示进行时）
3. B（三单 wears）
4. A（进行时用 be 提问、be 回答）
5. B（helps others 对应 helpful）
6. A（each other 固定短语）
7. C（否定句并列用 or）
8. A（主语 he 配 is）

**三、词形填空**
1. is swimming（双写 m）
2. is singing（someone 单数）
3. are making（去 e 加 ing）
4. friendly
5. runs; is sitting（双写 t）

**四、改写句子**
1. Are they playing chess now?
2. What is she doing?
3. He isn't watching TV at the moment.
4. My best friend is always there for me.

**五、汉译英**
1. What does your best friend look like? — She is tall and wears glasses.
2. Look! They are drawing (pictures) in the classroom.
3. We both like music, but our favourite singers are different.
4. He is funny and honest, and he always makes me laugh.

## 七、易错提醒

- 第 1 题：look like 问外貌、be like 问性格，答语决定选项。
- 词形填空第 5 题：前半句 usually 用一般现在时三单，后半句 now 用进行时，一题双考。
- 汉译英第 4 题：make sb laugh 中 laugh 用**动词原形**。
