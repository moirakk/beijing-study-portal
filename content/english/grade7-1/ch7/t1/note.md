# Fantastic friends（了不起的朋友）

标签：#Unit6 #朋友 #外貌性格描写

## 一、单元定位

外研版七年级上册 **Unit 6 Fantastic friends**，话题是"了不起的朋友"。学习描述朋友的外貌与性格，谈论彼此的共同点与不同点，理解真正的友谊。

## 四、语法讲解：形容词的用法与 look like / be like

1. **形容词的两个位置**：
   - 名词前作定语：a **tall** boy, **short** hair；
   - be 动词后作表语：He **is tall**. Her hair **is short**.
2. **描述外貌的两种句式**：
   - **be + 形容词**：She is tall and thin.
   - **have/has + 形容词 + 名词**：She **has** long black hair.（❌ Her hair is long and black 也对，但不要写 She is long hair）
3. **What does she look like?（问外貌）与 What is she like?（问性格/为人）**：
   - look like 答外貌：She's of medium height and wears glasses.
   - be like 答性格：She's outgoing and helpful.
4. **多个形容词排序**（初一常见）：大小/长短 + 颜色 + 名词：long black hair, big blue eyes。
5. **常见错误对比**：
   - ❌ She is long hair. → ✅ She **has** long hair. / Her hair **is** long.
   - ❌ a black long coat → ✅ a **long black** coat

## 五、听说读写要点

- **功能表达**：描述外貌（tall, short, thin, of medium height, hair, eyes, glasses）；描述性格（friendly, funny, quiet, outgoing, helpful, honest）；比较异同（both, same, different）。
- **写作模板**：My best friend is ... She/He is ... and has ... She/He is very ...(性格). We both like ..., but ... I'm lucky to have such a fantastic friend.
- 口语操练：Who is your best friend? / What does he look like? / What is he like? / Why do you like him?

## 六、考点与易错点

- **look like 问外貌 / be like 问性格**的区分是单选高频题。
- both 位置：be 动词后（We **are both** twelve）、实义动词前（We **both like** music）。
- friendly 虽以 -ly 结尾但是**形容词**：be friendly **to** sb。
- glasses（眼镜）恒为复数：wear glasses；a pair of glasses。

## 七、小练习

1. — What does your friend look like? — ______
   A. She's kind. B. She's tall and has long hair. C. She likes music.
2. — What's Tony like? — ______
   A. He's funny and helpful. B. He's tall. C. He likes football.
3. My cousin wears ______ and he has short ______ hair.（A. glasses; black B. glass; blacks C. glasses; blacks）
4. Lucy and I ______ like drawing, and we are in ______ same class.（A. both; a B. both; the C. all; the）
5. Amy is shy, ______ her sister is very outgoing.（A. and B. but C. or）
6. 汉译英：我最好的朋友对每个人都很友好，她总是逗我们笑。

**答案**：1. B（look like 问外貌）2. A（be like 问性格）3. A 4. B（the same 固定）5. B 6. My best friend is friendly to everyone. She always makes us laugh.
