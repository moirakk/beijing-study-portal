# More than fun（不只是乐趣——兴趣爱好）

标签：#Unit2 #兴趣爱好 #hobbies

## 一、单元定位

外研版七年级上册 **Unit 2 More than fun**，话题是"兴趣爱好"。爱好不只是玩：它带来快乐，也帮助我们学习、交友、成长。本单元学习谈论自己和他人的爱好，说明喜欢的原因。

## 四、语法讲解：like doing 与 play 的用法

1. **like / love / enjoy + doing**：表示喜欢做某事。
   - I like **swimming**. She loves **painting**. We enjoy **singing** together.
   - ❌ I like swim. → ✅ I like **swimming** / I like **to swim**.
2. **play 的两种搭配**（高频考点）：
   - play + **the** + 乐器：play **the** piano / **the** guitar / **the** violin；
   - play + 球类棋类（不加 the）：play football / basketball / chess。
   - ❌ play the football → ✅ play football；❌ play piano → ✅ play **the** piano。
3. **动词变 -ing 拼写**：
   - 直接加 ing：read → reading, sing → singing；
   - 去 e 加 ing：dance → dancing, make → making；
   - 双写尾字母加 ing：swim → swi**mm**ing, run → ru**nn**ing。

## 五、听说读写要点

- **功能表达**：询问与介绍爱好；说明爱好带来的好处（keep healthy, make friends, learn skills, help me relax）。
- **口语操练**：What do you do in your free time? / Why do you like it?
- **写作模板**：My hobby is ... I usually ... in my free time. It's more than fun, because it helps me ... I want to keep this hobby.

## 六、考点与易错点

- like doing 中动词 -ing 的**双写**（swimming, running）是词形变化高频考点。
- play 与 the 的取舍几乎每次考试都出现。
- be good at 后接名词或 **doing**：She is good at **dancing**（❌ dance）。
- makes me + 形容词：It makes me **happy**（❌ happily）。

## 七、小练习

1. My brother likes ______ (swim) in summer.
2. Lucy can play ______ piano, and she can also play ______ chess.（A. the; the B. the; / C. /; the）
3. I'm good at ______.（A. sing B. singing C. sings）
4. Painting ______ me relax after class.（A. helps B. help C. helping）
5. 汉译英：我的爱好是读书，它不只是乐趣，还帮助我学到很多知识。
6. 汉译英：你想加入音乐社团吗？

**答案**：1. swimming 2. B 3. B 4. A（主语 Painting 为动名词，作单数）5. My hobby is reading. It's more than fun, and it also helps me learn a lot. 6. Do you want to join the music club?
