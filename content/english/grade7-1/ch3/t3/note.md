# Unit 2·综合练习

标签：#Unit2 #单元测试 #综合练习

## 一、练习说明

覆盖 Unit 2 核心：爱好词汇、can 的用法、like doing、建议句型。建议限时 25 分钟。

## 二、单项选择

1. — What's your hobby? — ______
   A. I'm fine. B. My hobby is collecting stamps. C. Yes, I like it.
2. Amy can play ______ violin, but she can't play ______ basketball.
   A. the; the B. /; the C. the; /
3. My father enjoys ______ photos of birds.
   A. take B. takes C. taking
4. — ______ your sister swim? — No, she ______.
   A. Can; can't B. Do; don't C. Can; isn't
5. ______ makes me strong and healthy.
   A. Run B. Running C. Runs
6. — Let's ______ the chess club! — Good idea!
   A. join B. to join C. joining
7. Why not ______ something new this term?
   A. trying B. try C. tries
8. Hobbies are ______ fun. They also help us learn new skills.
   A. more than B. less than C. better than

## 三、用所给词的适当形式填空

1. Lingling is good at ______ (dance).
2. He can ______ (sing) many English songs.
3. How about ______ (swim) this Sunday?
4. ______ (read) books opens up a new world for me.
5. My brother likes ______ (run) in the morning.

## 四、按要求改写句子

1. Tony can play chess.（改为一般疑问句）→ ______
2. I like painting pictures.（改为否定句）→ ______
3. She can play the piano.（对画线部分提问，画线：play the piano）→ ______
4. free, what, do, in, your, you, time, do（连词成句）→ ______

## 五、汉译英

1. 我的爱好是游泳，它让我保持健康。
2. 你会弹钢琴吗？——不，我不会。
3. 咱们放学后去打篮球吧！
4. 爱好不只是乐趣，它还帮助我们交朋友。

## 六、参考答案

**二、单项选择**
1. B（问爱好答爱好）
2. C（乐器加 the，球类不加）
3. C（enjoy + doing）
4. A（can 的疑问与否定自成体系）
5. B（动名词作主语）
6. A（Let's + 动词原形）
7. B（Why not + 动词原形）
8. A（more than fun 不只是乐趣）

**三、词形填空**
1. dancing 2. sing 3. swimming（双写 m）4. Reading 5. running（双写 n）

**四、改写句子**
1. Can Tony play chess?
2. I don't like painting pictures.
3. What can she do?
4. What do you do in your free time?

**五、汉译英**
1. My hobby is swimming. It keeps me healthy.
2. Can you play the piano? — No, I can't.
3. Let's play basketball after school!
4. Hobbies are more than fun. They also help us make friends.

## 七、易错提醒

- 第 4 题：句中有 can，提问和回答都不用 do/don't。
- 词形填空第 3、5 题：swim、run 变 -ing 要**双写尾字母**。
- 汉译英第 2 题："弹钢琴"必须带 the：play **the** piano。
